DOMAIN 01

Interpersonal Skills

Develop the ability to interact effectively with others. Children learn to build positive relationships, work collaboratively, resolve conflicts, and understand communication cues, helping them navigate social situations with confidence and empathy.

Find a Program→
Better, together.

TOPICS WE EXPLORE

  • Collaboration

    Working together to achieve common goals.

  • Building Positive Relationships

    Initiating, developing, and maintaining healthy relationships.

  • Conflict Resolution

    Managing disagreements constructively.

  • Active Listening and Communication

    Listening attentively and communicating effectively.

  • Verbal and Nonverbal Cues

    Recognizing and interpreting communication signals.

  • Problem-Solving

    Identifying challenges and exploring possible solutions.

DOMAIN 02

Personal Development

Focus on self-improvement and personal growth. Children explore their values, set and work toward goals, build self-esteem, and learn to advocate for themselves, developing a positive self-image and a clear sense of direction.

Find a Program→
Room to grow.

TOPICS WE EXPLORE

  • Gratitude

    Recognizing and appreciating the good in life.

  • Self-Esteem

    Building a positive self-image and confidence.

  • Goal Setting and Self Reflection

    Identifying goals, planning steps, and reflecting on progress.

  • Values Exploration

    Understanding core values and aligning actions.

  • Self Advocacy

    Expressing needs, desires, and boundaries effectively.

DOMAIN 03

Emotional Well-Being

Focus on understanding and managing emotions. Children learn to identify their feelings, develop coping skills, practice mindfulness, and navigate emotional challenges with resilience and calmness.

TOPICS WE EXPLORE

  • Thinking Traps

    Recognizing and challenging unhelpful thought patterns.

  • Emotional Identification

    Naming and understanding emotions accurately.

  • Emotional Regulation

    Managing emotions in healthy and constructive ways.

  • Coping Skills

    Building strategies to handle stress and challenges.

  • Tolerating Frustration/Disappointment

    Developing resilience when things are hard.

  • Mindful Practices

    Using mindfulness to stay present and calm.
