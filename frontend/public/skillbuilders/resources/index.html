SMALL MOMENTS. MEANINGFUL PRACTICE.

Try it Together

Simple conversation starters to adapt with your child and program team.

Interpersonal Skills

Make a plan, together.

Take turns planning a shared activity. Practice listening to each idea, choosing a plan together, and asking for help when you need it.

Personal Development

Choose one small goal.

Choose one small goal for the week. Name a first step, celebrate the effort, and reflect together on what you learned.

Emotional Well-Being

Make space for feelings.

Make room for a daily feelings check-in. Name an emotion, notice how it feels in your body, and choose a calming activity together.

Who is Skill Builders for?+

Skill Builders is designed for children and young learners. Contact the team to discuss your child’s needs and find out which current programs may be a good fit.

What skills does the program cover?+

The program covers 17 topics across three domains: interpersonal skills, personal development, and emotional well-being. Explore the Program Overview for the full outline.

How can I find available programs?+

Use Find a Program to visit the Skill Builders program listing. The team can help you confirm availability, location, scheduling, and enrollment details.

Can families practice these skills at home?+

Yes. Everyday conversations, shared activities, and time for reflection offer opportunities to practice. The ideas on this page are simple starting points to adapt with your child and their program team.

How can a school learn more?+

Contact the ITSCO team with your school name and a brief description of what you are looking for. The team can discuss options and next steps with you.
