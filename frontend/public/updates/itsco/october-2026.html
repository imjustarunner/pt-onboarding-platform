Colorado Billing & Compensation Appendix

Clarify service pay, separate conditional Tier 3 additions, and distinguish clinical-credit sick-leave accrual from the ten-minute H-code indirect allowance.

Clinical Hour Rate currently covers approved 9-series clinical services at their assigned credit values. Standard Professional Rate currently covers approved H-code services at their documented hour or unit equivalents. Additional service codes may be assigned to either rate through a dated handbook update and an approved payroll mapping before use; a code prefix alone does not authorize a service or determine pay. Existing code-specific eligibility, indirect allowances and group restrictions continue to apply.

Clinical Hour Rate — approved 9-series services

Approved psychotherapy services, including 90832, 90834 and 90837, use the Clinical Hour Rate in the employee’s schedule. Family psychotherapy, psychotherapy add-ons, crisis services and group psychotherapy use their approved code-specific credit values and applicable authorization requirements. The “top” service rate is a category, not a promise of a full credit for every code. A 1.00-credit service pays the full credit rate; a 0.75-credit service pays 75% of that rate. No additional 20% indirect cash line is added to a clinical service-credit encounter.

Standard Professional Rate: H-code units, encounters and the EHR transition

Current method while using the external EHR. Payroll tabulates the documented services from the external record. Use the employee’s applicable Standard Professional Rate and the code-specific basis below. A payer encounter is not automatically one hour of employee pay.

Fifteen-minute unit boundaries

Where the applicable payer uses Colorado’s published 15-minute midpoint table: 8 through 22 minutes = 1 unit; 23 through 37 minutes = 2 units; 38 through 52 minutes = 3 units; 53 through 67 minutes = 4 units. Thus 23 minutes is two units, not one. Verify the payer, service-date rules, modifiers and authorized scope before billing; this compensation policy does not authorize a claim.

Included Indirect Service allowance

Integrated Services include Indirect Service time equal to 20% of compensated Direct Service time or its assigned time-equivalent, unless the Compensation Schedule expressly specifies another allowance. One direct hour includes 12 indirect minutes; 15 direct minutes include 3 indirect minutes. The included component is paid at the employee’s applicable Indirect Service Rate. Report qualifying excess work without duplicate payment.

Worked examples

At a $32 Standard Professional Rate and $22 Indirect Service Rate, one verified 15-minute compensation unit earns $8.00 direct and $1.10 included indirect; four units earn $32.00 direct and $4.40 included indirect. An encounter assigned 30 direct minutes earns $16.00 direct and $2.20 included indirect. Apply the service-specific Compensation Schedule and retain full precision until payroll aggregation.

Service-specific compensation method

The Compensation Schedule identifies whether each Integrated Service uses actual minutes, verified units, or a fixed encounter value. A software transition alone does not change the compensation method. Retain the method and effective date for each service date, including late-entered services.

Actual work and sick leave

Track actual direct, indirect and support work separately. Compensation units are not a substitute for actual-hours records. A service below a payer’s billing threshold or a delayed or denied claim does not erase pay for compensable work; report it for the appropriate payroll treatment. Payroll must reconcile minimum wage, overtime and other applicable wage requirements against actual work time. H-code direct compensation equivalents and paid indirect components enter the sick-leave calculation once; do not add the clinical-credit 1.20 multiplier. Reconcile against actual compensable hours and add any sick-leave accrual required by Colorado law.

Reference: Colorado behavioral health time documentation and unit rules. Billing must verify the current payer-specific requirements for the service date.

Actual time, payer units and internal credits

Record exact actual direct, indirect and support minutes separately from payer claim units and compensation credits. A qualifying 90837 maps to 1.00 compensation credit even when actual minutes differ from 60. Billing must follow the current payer’s requirements; a 53–74 minute example is not a universal maximum-duration rule. Verify scope, supervision, modifiers, place of service and telehealth rules for each service date. Never use leave-equivalent hours as actual hours worked.

Any service delivered or billed as a group, regardless of its code, is subject to the group moratorium and requires express written approval before use. Probation and transition

Probation is at most 90 calendar days from original start, ends on an earlier manual waiver or achievement of Tier 3, and never restarts after Tier 3. Probation uses the disclosed minimum-workload rate in each pay category. Minimum-workload reductions are waived for 60 calendar days after the amendment’s prospective effective date. An October 10 effective date is protected through December 8; the reduction can first apply December 9. The waiver does not restart or extend probation. Track service dates across every rate boundary.

Versioned changes

Maintain the clinical-credit mappings, H-code indirect percentage and sick-leave ratio in the dated appendix. State the effective service date and provide written notice, plus agreement where required, before affected work. Preserve prior tables and signed schedules for late-entered services. Handbook edits alone do not enable payroll or reduce signed personal rates.

Category and level rate schedule

Clarify service pay, separate conditional Tier 3 additions, and distinguish clinical-credit sick-leave accrual from the ten-minute H-code indirect allowance.

Category and level rate schedule

Clinical Hour Rate
The assigned credit value multiplied by your clinical rate. This covers approved 9-series clinical codes and designated master’s-level services in the appendix; it does not authorize every code beginning with 9. A 1.00-credit service pays the full rate and a 0.75-credit service pays 75%. Routine administrative work is included in this service pay. The 1.20 equivalent affects sick-leave accrual, not additional cash pay.
Standard Professional Rate
The rate used to calculate the Direct Service component of an Integrated Service from the service-specific minutes, units, or time-equivalent. The included indirect allowance is 20% of that direct time-equivalent, paid at the applicable Indirect Service Rate, unless the Compensation Schedule specifies another allowance.
Indirect Service Rate
The hourly rate used for the included indirect component and separately compensable qualifying Indirect Service. Do not submit the same included work twice. Qualifying excess work remains reportable and payable.
Support activity pay
The hourly rate for designated support activities, including required Provider Update review. Record actual compensable time.
Probationary and minimum-workload rates
Two distinct conditions that use the stated reduced rate: new-hire probation and the minimum-workload policy. Each has its own timing and exceptions, as described in the current Introductory Compensation and Minimum Workload Rate policies. A slash in the prior chart separated H-code and clinical rates; it did not combine them into one payment.
Levels and conditional bonuses
A compensation level selects the base rate within the credential category; it is separate from a workload/benefit tier. The chart’s “+” amount is conditional, not guaranteed and not included in base pay. The addition applies only when the employee qualifies for Tier 3 based on sessions per pay period.

Category 1 · Unlicensed

Unlicensed staff. The H-code row applies to eligible bachelor’s-level services. The clinical service-credit row applies only to authorized intern/unlicensed master’s-level services. Scope and supervision requirements still apply. Integrated Services include the 20% indirect allowance unless a service-specific exception is stated in the Compensation Schedule. Report qualifying excess indirect work separately.

Conditional Tier 3 additions

Tier 3 currently requires 50 qualifying session credits per two-week pay period. Current-period session credits determine this addition; retained benefit tiers during grace do not qualify.

This addition is conditional compensation governed by the current, effective Workplace Handbook schedule for your category and level; it is separate from your agreed base rate. Earned additions for earlier work remain payable under the policy then in effect.

Record changes in the app’s pay-system rate settings with their prospective effective date and give staff notice. Changing handbook prose alone does not recalculate payroll.

Compensation levels: expectations, good standing and review

Make compensation, service approval and benefit rules easy to find and understand.

This section establishes the shared considerations for qualifying for, maintaining and reviewing compensation levels. The employee’s completed individual schedule identifies the assigned category, level and rates. These expectations take effect prospectively under the employee’s amendment and written notice; they do not create undisclosed retroactive criteria.

Annual paid event-work commitment

I agree to participate in a minimum of four (4) hours of assigned outreach or agency events during each calendar year, January 1 through December 31, compensated separately at my agreed-upon indirect hourly rate. While school-assigned, the annual commitment is eight (8) hours total, including four (4) additional hours for school-related events. The eight-hour total applies across all school assignments, not separately to each school. Actual event time is recorded and paid separately from service-credit compensation and automatic H-code indirect allowances; the same work may not be counted or paid twice.

ITSCO and the employee will identify and schedule the indirect duties that satisfy this commitment, including assigned outreach, community and school events. Required outreach under this commitment is paid indirect work, not unpaid volunteering. Other activities retain the pay classification disclosed in the compensation schedule unless expressly designated as part of this indirect-work assignment. The commitment does not automatically add four or eight hours to payroll: record all actual time worked, including additional compensable time. ITSCO will make assignments and scheduling expectations clear. Management will address unavailable assignments, school closures, approved schedule changes, protected leave and reasonable accommodations; an employee will not be treated as failing this commitment solely because ITSCO did not make the work available. Requirements are adjusted as required for protected leave and accommodations, without unpaid make-up work. Applicable minimum wage and overtime protections continue to apply.

Evaluation categories

1. Annual paid event-work commitment

Complete the assigned four-hour annual event commitment, or eight-hour annual total while school-assigned (January 1–December 31), with accurate actual-time records and timely follow-through. Review both the work made available and the work performed; do not substitute automatic payroll allowances for actual participation.

Review evidence: Assigned duties, agreed schedules, time records and completed work, with approved adjustments documented.

2. Documentation quality and timeliness

Complete accurate notes, treatment plans and required documentation within the communicated clinical, payer and agency deadlines. Correct errors and respond to documentation requests promptly. Review system outages, approved extensions and protected absences before identifying a performance concern.

Review evidence: Applicable deadlines, completion records, accuracy reviews and the employee’s explanation of barriers.

3. Attendance, call-offs and clinician cancellations

Meet agreed schedules, give timely notice when unable to attend, and support appropriate client notification, rescheduling and coverage. Consider avoidable clinician cancellations and unprotected attendance concerns in context. Exclude protected leave and accommodations; distinguish clinician decisions from client cancellations, school closures and events outside the employee’s control.

Review evidence: Scheduling and notice records, reasons for cancellations, coverage efforts and confirmed circumstances.

4. Outreach and team participation

Participate in assigned outreach events and practice or school partnership activities that fit the employee’s role and disclosed schedule. Communicate conflicts and complete agreed alternatives when appropriate. Assigned outreach hours under this commitment are paid at the agreed indirect rate and count toward the four- or eight-hour calendar-year total; additional actual work remains compensable.

Review evidence: Event assignments, participation, completed follow-up and documented scheduling arrangements.

5. Professional standing, collaboration and feedback

Maintain required credentials and supervision, follow professional and agency standards, communicate respectfully and address substantiated concerns. Consider reports from schools, clients and staff after reviewing facts, context and the employee’s response. A complaint alone is not a finding of misconduct or an automatic basis for a lower level. Good-faith reporting, wage concerns and other protected activity are not adverse factors.

Review evidence: Credential and supervision records, specific observed conduct, documented feedback and reviewed findings.

6. Client connection and engagement

Build a responsive, respectful therapeutic relationship; communicate appropriately with clients and authorized caregivers; and address access, language and engagement barriers within the employee’s role. Evaluate the clinician’s actions and quality of care, with attention to client choice and confidentiality, rather than popularity or retention alone.

Review evidence: Appropriate feedback, communication and follow-up records, care coordination and documented engagement efforts.

7. Treatment goals, progress and appropriate completion of care

Develop individualized goals and objectives, assess progress, adjust care when needed, and support clinically appropriate goal attainment, termination, transfer or referral. Consider client acuity, readiness, disability, school context and other barriers. Progress is a consideration, not a guaranteed outcome. Do not reward unnecessary treatment, premature discharge, selecting only easier cases or a predetermined number of successful terminations.

Review evidence: Clinical review of treatment planning, progress assessment, appropriate adjustments and continuity-of-care decisions.

8. School and client action items

Complete assigned intake, family contact, school coordination, roster, scheduling and other client action items by communicated deadlines. Keep status records accurate and escalate blocked items promptly. Apply school-specific responsibilities only while school-assigned and within authorized information-sharing boundaries.

Review evidence: Task assignments, due dates, completion records, escalations and documented external dependencies.

Evaluation schedule and good standing

ITSCO will conduct formal compensation-level evaluations one to two times per year. Supervisors may provide feedback and address concerns between formal evaluations. Reviews consider the responsibilities applicable to the employee’s role, the expectations disclosed in advance, reliable evidence and the employee’s response. Good standing means substantially meeting the applicable professional, documentation, reliability, participation and follow-through expectations, with concerns assessed fairly and in context. Protected leave, reasonable accommodations and other protected activity are excluded from adverse consideration.

For each applicable category, document “Meets expectations,” “Needs improvement” or “Not applicable,” with the review period, supporting facts and the employee’s comments. Disclose role- or level-specific standards before using them. This framework does not establish undisclosed point totals, weighting or automatic promotion/demotion thresholds.

Maintaining or changing a compensation level

A review may result in maintaining the current compensation level, advancement when the applicable criteria are met, or a prospective reduction within the employee’s compensation category when documented expectations are not met. Advancement is not automatic. Management will communicate the reasons, relevant evidence and expectations for improvement, ordinarily with a written improvement plan and a follow-up date; serious concerns may require earlier action. The employee may submit a response or request management review. Any changed level and resulting rates must be communicated in writing before affected work, with any agreement required by existing terms. No review reduces pay or leave already earned. A handbook edit, complaint, missed task or app-generated flag does not automatically change an employee’s pay level. Credential categories and session-based workload tiers remain distinct from performance-based compensation levels; Tier 3 bonus eligibility continues to follow the disclosed session-count rules.

Clinical decisions must prioritize appropriate care and client choice. Reviewers must not use treatment outcomes or discharge counts as quotas. Share only the information appropriate for the review; detailed clinical records remain in the authorized clinical record.

Supervisory review and evaluations

Supervisory reviews, clinical documentation reviews and formal performance evaluations are also considered when determining whether an employee meets the expectations for their compensation level. Findings must be documented, reviewed in context and discussed with the employee.

Sick-leave accrual ratio for fee-for-service staff

Clarify annual paid event participation, current sick-leave rates, activity classifications and prospective compensation policies.

Initial ratio: 20% administrative equivalent

Clinical credits × 1.20 ÷ 30 = sick-leave hours earned. The direct bucket receives 1.00 equivalent hour per credit and the administrative bucket receives 0.20. Thus 25 credits = 30 equivalent hours = 1 hour of sick leave; 0.75 credit = 0.90 equivalent hour = 0.03 sick-leave hour. The administrative equivalent affects accrual only for psychotherapy credit services and is already included in service pay.

Integrated Direct Service time and its included Indirect Service time enter the accrual calculation once. Under the standard 20% allowance, one direct hour plus 0.20 included indirect hour equals 1.20 accrual hours. Do not apply another clinical-credit multiplier to that total. Separately compensated Indirect Service, Support Activity, and qualifying Excess Time also count without duplication. Reconcile against all actual compensable work and apply any required sick-leave shortfall.

This remains protected paid sick leave, not a new vacation bank. It accrues without a probation waiting period and is paid using the highest currently eligible rate under the sick-leave pay policy. Payroll applies the HFWA calculation rules for the leave dates, using the currently applicable clinical per-credit or direct hourly base rate, excluding Tier 3 additions and other bonuses or differentials. Preserve existing balances and more generous earned terms. HFWA generally requires at least one hour per 30 hours worked up to 48 annually, with applicable carryover up to 48 hours; protected uses, reasonable notice, permitted documentation and nonretaliation requirements apply. Do not apply a generic 10-hour rollover cap or 30-day notice rule to protected sick leave.

ITSCO may update the ratio prospectively through the dated handbook appendix and corresponding payroll policy version, with notice and any required agreement. Preserve accrued balances and historical service calculations. AI documentation does not change the ratio automatically or remove pay for required review and correction. Actual work remains compensable even with a zero allowance. Any separate vacation/general PTO entitlement retains its existing protections.

School mileage reimbursement

Make compensation, service approval and benefit rules easy to find and understand.

This policy outlines how eligible employees may request and receive mileage reimbursement for travel to approved school locations. Mileage reimbursement is a discretionary benefit, not a guaranteed entitlement, and is governed by the Practice’s Benefit Tier Framework and operational considerations.

Eligibility, reimbursement rates, and caps may be modified or discontinued at any time.

Eligibility & Tier Levels

Mileage reimbursement eligibility is determined by the employee’s assigned Benefit Tier at the time of submission:

  • Tier 1: No mileage reimbursement
  • Tier 2: $0.35 per eligible mile
  • Tier 3: $0.70 per eligible mile

Mileage reimbursement is subject to an annual reimbursement cap of $2,000 per employee, unless otherwise approved in writing. This cap may be adjusted, suspended, or eliminated at the Practice’s discretion.

Claim Process

Record the travel date, assigned school, originating office, eligible route, and miles in My Payroll → Mileage. Use the school-travel option and assigned office; explain any manual-distance correction. Submit by the Sunday after the pay period closes. Other business mileage requires prior approval.

Payroll reviews the applicable tier and eligible miles. A zero optional benefit rate does not remove reimbursement required by law. Request review of a missing route, incorrect distance, or eligibility decision.

Calculation Method

  • Commute Deduction: Reimbursement is calculated based on the roundtrip distance from the employee’s home to the assigned school location minus the roundtrip distance from the employee’s home to their designated office location (Windchime or Broadway).
  • School Locations Only: Only services that actually occurred at school sites are eligible under this policy.
  • Multiple School Locations: If an employee travels to multiple schools in a single day, reimbursement is calculated using only the single school farthest from the employee’s home. Additional travel between schools is not separately reimbursable.
  • Closer Locations: If the assigned school is closer to the employee’s home than the designated office, no mileage reimbursement will be issued for that trip.

Payment & Processing

  • Payroll Integration: Approved mileage reimbursement will be processed through the employee’s regular paycheck.
  • Tax Treatment: Mileage reimbursement is treated as taxable income and subject to applicable payroll taxes.
  • Paystub Display: Approved amounts will appear as “Mileage Reimbursement” on the employee’s paystub.

Example

An employee lives 12 miles roundtrip from the Windchime office.

The assigned school is 17 miles roundtrip from the employee’s home.

Difference: 5 miles

At Tier 2 (50%):

5 miles × $0.35 = $1.75

At Tier 3 (100%):

5 miles × $0.70 = $3.50

Compliance and Oversight

Employees are responsible for accurate mileage reporting and ensuring that all addresses used for calculation are correct. Mileage claims must align with EHR-documented services.

Any misrepresentation or inconsistency may result in denial of reimbursement and/or corrective action.

Modification or Discontinuation

ITSCO may change mileage eligibility, rates, caps, or availability prospectively with notice. Changes do not remove reimbursement already earned or required by law.
