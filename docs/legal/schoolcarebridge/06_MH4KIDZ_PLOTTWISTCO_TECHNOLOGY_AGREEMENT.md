## 2. Separate legal and data roles

MH4Kidz operates the program only to the extent confirmed in its governing documents and
customer contracts. PlotTwistCo supplies technology and does not become the school, treating
practice or nonprofit merely by hosting the application. A subcontractor BAA is required
where Provider processes PHI on behalf of Customer acting as a business associate; a direct
BAA is used where a covered entity directly engages Provider. Education-record processing
requires applicable school instructions and restrictions. Contractual role labels must
match actual access and services.

## 3. Processing restrictions and security

Use information only for documented lawful service instructions. Bind approved vendors to
required confidentiality, security, use, return and deletion obligations. Do not sell records,
create donor or advertising audiences from service data, train general-purpose AI models
on identifiable records, or broaden data use through a public website-policy update.
Apply the attached security controls, incident response, monitoring, backup, business
continuity and change-control requirements. Notify Customer within the incident period in
the controlling BAA/DPA; do not wait for a complete investigation before an initial report.
Material feature, subcontractor, location or data-use changes require the agreed review process.

## 4. Pricing, conflicts and charitable assets

Complete a written fee schedule specifying the work, rate/basis, payment periods, pass-through

actual services. Identify common directors, officers, owners or other financial interests.

privacy duties or organizational separation. Have the nonprofit's tax adviser determine
