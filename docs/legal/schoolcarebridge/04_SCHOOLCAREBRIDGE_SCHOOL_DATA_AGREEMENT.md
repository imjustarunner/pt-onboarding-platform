## 1. Authorized service and disclosure basis

The service is the specific school coordination function in Schedule 1. School shall
identify the applicable basis before disclosing student information: valid consent or a
specified FERPA exception, together with every condition of that exception. This contract
alone does not create permission to disclose records.

If School relies on the school-official exception, School determines and documents that
the outsourced function is one for which it would otherwise use employees, that the
service provider meets its annual-notice criteria, and that access is limited to legitimate
educational interests. Provider is under School's direct control regarding the use and
maintenance of those education records, shall comply with School's instructions, and
shall permit School to monitor and enforce those limits. If those conditions are not met,
use an appropriate consent or another valid basis before disclosure.

## 2. Data minimization and access

List permitted fields, user groups, participating organizations and purposes in Schedule 1.
No general student directory, unrelated roster, complete clinical chart, psychotherapy
notes, or donor database is authorized by default. Each user receives an individual account,
verified affiliation and least-privilege access. School approves access, reviews it at
least each school term and on role changes, and requests prompt termination when it ends.
Provider shall implement those decisions and document access changes.

Use student information only for the contracted school purpose. No sale, targeted advertising,
independent fundraising, unauthorized profiling, unrelated product development, or training
of a general-purpose AI model on identifiable student information. No third-party disclosure
or redisclosure without School's lawful instruction and applicable permission. School's
instructions cannot authorize a disclosure prohibited by law.

## 3. Clinical records and family permissions

School education records and a health provider's independently maintained clinical records
are evaluated separately. The provider remains responsible for its HIPAA and other applicable
confidentiality duties. A BAA with a technology vendor does not authorize School to receive
clinical records. Use a specific lawful disclosure basis and any required parent, guardian,
eligible-student or patient authorization for each direction of exchange. Respect limits
on representative access and specially protected information. A portal login or referral
is not a general release. Record the source and scope of every applicable permission.

## 4. Security, vendors and incidents

Provider shall maintain appropriate access controls, encryption, workforce confidentiality,
logs, secure exports, response procedures, recovery and disposal protections. Complete the
control inventory and incident contacts in Schedule 2 before access. Provider shall list
approved subcontractors and bind them to the same applicable use, access, security,
redisclosure and deletion restrictions before providing data. Give School advance notice
of material changes and a reasonable means to object, stop affected processing or transition.

Notify School without unreasonable delay and within 24 hours of discovering suspected
unauthorized access or disclosure or a material security incident affecting its information.
Provide preliminary facts, preserve evidence, contain harm and update School as facts are
confirmed. Cooperate with lawful notices and remedies. This proposed contractual period
does not lengthen any applicable statutory deadline. No individual student data goes into
ordinary support email, donor reports or public incident announcements.

## 5. Requests, records and oversight

Refer parent/eligible-student access, correction and complaint requests to School unless
law requires otherwise, and assist School within ten business days or sooner to meet an
identified deadline. Separately route clinical-record requests to the treating organization.
School may review appropriate compliance evidence and investigate suspected misuse without
receiving another organization's records or credentials. Maintain a record of authorized
instructions, data transfers and applicable disclosures.

## 6. Retention, termination and return

School specifies retention and deletion in Schedule 2, subject to legal obligations and
holds. At service end, provide a usable export within the agreed period and delete service
copies and subcontractor copies as agreed. Document retained backups, legal holds, access
restrictions and the date remaining copies expire. No independent use of retained copies.
Provide completion or exception certification. Access may be suspended immediately for
misuse or a material risk; School may terminate for material breach. A reasonable transition
must protect lawful access and avoid abandonment of records.

## 7. Relationship, fees and signatures

This is a data-use agreement, not a clinical treatment consent, medical release, SMS opt-in,
recording consent or student waiver of rights. Fees, service availability and support are
in the identified service agreement. No website revision silently broadens the signed
scope. Changes require authorized written approval. Mandatory law controls a conflict;
otherwise this agreement controls less protective general website terms for school data.
