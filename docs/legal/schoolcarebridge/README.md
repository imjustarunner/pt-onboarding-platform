certification of compliance. Have qualified healthcare/school-data and nonprofit counsel

Select agreements from the real data/contract chain; do not sign every document indiscriminately.
SchoolCareBridge and AuricWell are service names. The legal entities sign contracts. A nonprofit
is not automatically a HIPAA covered entity or business associate because it is tax-exempt.
If MH4Kidz never receives, maintains or transmits PHI on a practice's behalf, reassess whether
its BAA is needed instead of giving it an artificial data role. A hosted cloud service can be
a business associate even if staff do not routinely read the records it maintains.

## What must be completed

1. Exact legal names, formation types, addresses and authorized signers for PlotTwistCo and
   MH4Kidz; verify the latter's tax classification. Do not infer “LLC” or “Inc.” from a brand.
2. Align existing contracts with the confirmed structure: MH4Kidz supplies the program and
   PlotTwistCo supplies technology. Document whose instructions each processes, any direct
   practice contracts, and the actual subprocessor chain.
3. Actual data fields, transfer directions, features, hosting locations, vendors and permitted
   users. Separate school records, coordination records and clinical records.
4. School/district disclosure basis and required permissions, plus existing practice releases.
5. Fees, term, support, liability allocation and privacy/security contacts. Proposed 24-hour
   incident reports, five-business-day breach reports and retention periods must be feasible
   and affirmatively adopted before signing; these are draft business terms.
6. Security, account and vendor verification, exit/export/deletion tests, and nonprofit conflict
   review. Use an approved electronic-signature process that retains the exact signed text.

## Website notices versus signed agreements

School staff should see SchoolCareBridge use/privacy terms even when entering through an
ITSCO hostname, along with relevant practice notices. AuricWell users should see platform
use/privacy terms plus their practice's notices. This update supplies notices and links;
it does not claim to record organization-level contractual acceptance from a page view.

Do not require school employees to sign a BAA on behalf of their employers. Use authorized
organization signers. An SMS choice, a HIPAA-notice acknowledgment, a treatment consent,
a release authorization and a BAA serve different purposes. Keep them distinct.

## Research basis

- [HHS: Business associates](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html): roles depend on the actual function and PHI processing.
- [HHS: BAA provisions](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/sample-business-associate-agreement-provisions/index.html) and [45 CFR 164.504](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-E/section-164.504): contract subject matter. Draft language here is tailored, not a representation that HHS approved this agreement.
- [45 CFR 164.410](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-D/section-164.410): business-associate breach notification; drafts propose shorter contractual reporting.
- [Education/HHS joint guidance](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/resources/joint-guidance-application-ferpa-and-hipaa-student-health-records): distinguish education and clinical records.
- [Education: school-official agreements](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/faq/must-school-have-written-agreement-or-contract-community-based-organization-which-it-non): FERPA does not universally require a written contract for this exception, but it can support the required direct control; other laws/policies may require one.
- [IRS: private benefit/inurement](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/inurement-private-benefit-charitable-organizations) and [excess-benefit transactions](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/intermediate-sanctions-excess-benefit-transactions): nonprofit transactions require more than common ownership and a privacy agreement.
