# Scheduling audit — September 22, 2026

## Findings and changes

- Sign-in verification is the session-security check, not a second SSO login. Added a retry path when verification cannot finish, and corrected the platform briefing heading contrast.
- Dashboard and login briefing meeting cards distinguish upcoming meetings from current/next-five-minute meetings, with prominent Join buttons and exact-event schedule links.
- Removed the dashboard Documentation Alerts card. Its count came from submitted public-form documents awaiting client assignment; the documents remain available in Submitted Documents.
- Interview start times were stored correctly in UTC. Reminder-plan timestamps now explicitly carry UTC, avoiding a six-hour browser interpretation error. Hiring invitation receipts and candidates now appear alongside staff invitation/reminder history.
- Added Interview, Leadership Circle, and Supervisors meeting editor choices, agency defaults, and per-meeting agenda/goals/actions/attendance/transcription/screen-sharing settings. Leadership and supervisor meetings require admin/superadmin scheduling. Only administrators can change compensation defaults or override pay eligibility settings.
- Participant controls expose RSVP, required/optional attendance, cohost status, and administrator-visible compensation eligibility. Selected chips use names/photos. Departed attendees with recorded time stay under attended, rather than being classified as absent.
- New team-meeting invitations/reminders use tenant email delivery, host/cohost Reply-To, participant RSVP links, and included agenda/goals/action items. Default reminders are three weekdays before, 24 hours before, and five minutes before. Interview candidates retain their guest access boundary; RSVP cannot promote them to hosts.
- Meaningful team-meeting changes are delayed five minutes. Reverted changes and type/notes-only edits do not send change notices. Editing and drag/move confirmation include Notify participants. Delivery receipts prevent successful recipients from being replayed on retry, and series changes share a delivery batch.
- Compensation uses recorded attendance, effective-dated salary exclusion, administrator exclusion, and a supervisor-meeting rate derived from 50% of the individual-supervision rate. Existing reviewed/paid claims are preserved; event locks prevent concurrent duplicate claims.
- Weekly open slots retain their source agency, row identity, recurrence, and minute precision. The editor updates the existing owned row. Unrelated office affiliation no longer filters the tenant list for virtual availability. The office-request panel uses the available width, and an existing office booking has an explicit link action.
- Virtual-working-hours rows represent ongoing weekly availability. The editor no longer offers an unsupported one-time setting or displays an invented prebuilt end date for those rows.
