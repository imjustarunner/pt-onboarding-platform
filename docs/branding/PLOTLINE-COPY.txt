Reference privacy wording
The applicant receives completion status and the referee's name only.
Answers, ratings, results and internal contact notes stay confidential and
are available only to staff with the company's hiring permission.
Explain this to the referee before submission and in the thank-you email.

Reference emails use the verified Plotline reference sender and the company's
configured People Operations Reply-To. Marketing cards link to the general
Plotline signup page without any candidate ID, reference ID or bearer token.
