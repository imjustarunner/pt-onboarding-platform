BILLING WORKFLOW RELEASE — IMPLEMENTATION AND VERIFICATION
November 3, 2026 / America/Denver

Release/deployment evidence is maintained separately in the task deliverable.
This file describes implementation; it is not evidence of live acceptance.

REQUEST COVERAGE
1–2. One server-scoped work queue joins claims, signed notes, note drafts and past
sessions without notes. Stages: Note needed, Draft, Pending review, Revisions
needed, Ready to submit, Submitted, Processing, Paid, Denied, On hold and Closed.
Future scheduled visits remain in the selected client's history, outside the
claim queue. Draft/review/revision items have no claim submission actions.
Final eligible signatures, supervisor signatures and approved amended versions
now hand off to billing. Approved historical notes missing a billing record can
be reconciled with the idempotent dry-run/apply script. It never signs a note or
transmits a claim. Clinical approval and payer/coverage readiness remain separate.

3–6,18. All claims is the default across authorized organizations, with phase
buttons, server sorting, search, selectable columns and tenant sorting. Default
columns include service date, patient, service, clinician and supervisor/billed-as.
Patient links open billing; provider links open the provider. POS, diagnoses,
units and charge are available. Claim/progress review opens a visible dialog.
Patient history is explicitly scoped; it does not repopulate the global queue
with future scheduled services. Rejected-claim retry, payer replacement and void
create linked claim records preserving the original. Official Claim.MD frequency
values 1/7/8 and payer ICN/DCN are used with payer-specific readiness checks.

7–10,14–15. Primary-code ranges, age/service coverage, bachelor F-code exclusion,
supervisor authorization for non-F primary diagnoses, per-diagnosis justification,
autism/ADHD evidence, persistent unspecified-code alerts and adjustment-disorder
event-month review are enforced. Uploaded records OR a qualified clinician's own
documented evaluation can support autism/ADHD. No diagnosis is silently replaced.
90791/H0031 editors and generation preserve ordered diagnoses and their rationale.
See ../clinical-diagnosis-billing-policy-2026-11-03.txt for primary sources,
exceptions, catalog dates, audit/encryption and clinical-policy limits.

11. Appointment alerts have encrypted revision history and actor-bound
acknowledgements. Authorized billing/admin/supervisor/provider can set them.
Current alerts must be acknowledged before starting/continuing note entry.
They remain appointment information rather than claim metadata. Clinical
diagnosis flags appear in the record, note workspace and appointment schedule.

12–13. Providers see insurer names, responsible parties and payment-method
status, without charges, balances, claim messages or financial actions. A branded
FundThred QR links to authenticated, tenant-scoped Stripe setup when needed.
Primary, secondary and legacy Medicaid signals suppress payment requests.
Billing reviews actual recipients before explicitly sending a setup request.
Email uses the organization's verified billing sender/reply address; family
in-app notice and email precede the provider QR follow-up. Recipient and coverage
state is rechecked during sending. Persistent request keys prevent duplicate
messages on retry/reload. Sent, queued, held, test-redirected and unknown are
distinct. A timeout is not permission to resend. Adding a card does not authorize
a charge or recurring collection.

16. Billing transaction history is searchable, sortable and linked to claims.
17. Insurance-card requests collapse; View card opens a visible modal with its
own loading state and cleanup rather than an off-screen preview.
19–20. Insurance verification has a dedicated billing queue, exact-client task
links and client-profile access. Primary and secondary coverage are reviewed.
Existing OCR/eligibility intake automation is reused; saving changed insurance
queues a new verification job when configured. Verification is required before a
new provider assignment or Exchange posting. Authorized billers can verify from
the warning; others receive an actionable message. Existing assignments are
preserved. Public intake saves the request and provider preference while deferring
assignment, instead of losing the request when verification is pending.
