Testing component compensation and eligibility
Policy version: medicaid-testing-2026-11-13

Applies across practices, including ITSCO, Next Level Up and Inner Strength.
This policy is prospective through an executed individual compensation schedule.
It does not rewrite an earlier signed agreement, change earned pay, update a
payer fee schedule, grant clinical scope or authorize a claim.

PAY BUCKETS
96116/96121: neurobehavioral status examination, initial/additional hour.
Clinical Session Rate per actual eligible hour-equivalent; this family has a
narrower provider list. Interns require a licensed psychologist supervisor.
A master's credential alone does not establish this code's rendering eligibility.
96130/96131: psychological evaluation, integration, interpretation, report and
feedback. Existing Clinical Session Rate per actual eligible hour-equivalent.
96132/96133: the corresponding separately authorized neuropsychological work.
One actual evaluation hour equals 1 clinical credit. A claim unit does not
automatically equal one credit. No second automatic indirect cash allowance.

96136/96137: qualified-professional administration/scoring, two or more tests.
96138/96139: technician administration/scoring only through its own covered
pathway. Both use existing Direct Care hourly pay for actual component minutes,
plus the agreed indirect allowance (10 minutes per direct hour, prorated,
preserving a greater agreement). Thirty actual minutes equal .5 Direct Care
hour. Do not represent technician work as professional work to obtain payment.

96146: one automated instrument, automated result only. Actual employee setup
or support uses the approved Direct Care assignment; unattended computer time
is not employee time. Professional/technician administration and two or more
tests do not fit this code. Verify the separate benefit/provider pathway.

98966/98967/98968: eligible clinical telephone assessment/management, respectively
5-10, 11-20 and 21-30 minutes. Encounter claim quantity; actual clinical minutes
for Direct Care compensation and prorated indirect allowance. Established
member/parent/guardian and related-encounter exclusions apply. Feedback already
included in an evaluation cannot also be a telephone claim.

H0001: substance-use assessment, encounter billing; Direct Care pay for actual
minutes under a newly adopted schedule. It is not a general psychological test.
H0004: treatment-plan counseling. Preserve its existing verified 15-minute unit
mapping (.25 Direct Care hour/unit) and existing billing controls.

Routine scheduling, billing, authorization administration and other separately
approved administrative duties use indirect pay. Clinical interpretation and
report preparation belong to the evaluation component even without the member
present. Record actual compensable work once; no overlapping or duplicate time.
An unfavorable margin does not permit reclassifying clinical work as indirect
or reducing agreed wages for already performed work.

CLAIM TIMING IS SEPARATE FROM PAYROLL TIME
The October 2026 Colorado SBHS Manual lists hour-based evaluation families and
30-minute administration/scoring families. They are not arbitrary one-unit
encounters. Add-ons are 96131 with 96130, 96133 with 96132, 96137 with 96136,
and 96139 with 96138. Do not book an add-on as an independent primary service.
Common midpoint illustrations: 31-90 cumulative minutes for an hourly initial
unit, 91-150 for initial plus first add-on; 16-45 for a half-hour initial unit,
46-75 for initial plus first add-on. Apply the actual current payer episode,
date and unit rules; never derive claim quantities from payroll credits.
Neither the manual's increment maximum nor claim thresholds cap paid work time.

ELIGIBILITY
Psychological/neuropsychological Testing requires:
- A signed, effective Testing service role with documented instrument-specific
  qualifications, assigned components, responsible professional and supervision.
- An explicit active provider assignment to the applicable component/base code.
  An administration assignment does not grant evaluation or neuro interpretation.
- Medicaid client coverage for this policy, plus verified benefit, rendering
  provider type, authorization and modifier requirements before a claim.

H0001/H0004 and eligible telephone services retain their own clinical approvals;
they are not restricted to Testing staff merely because this reference lists them.
Colorado C.R.S. 12-245-232 and applicable board rules still control scope. An
internal role, degree, compensation category or another person's license is
insufficient by itself. Supervised administration does not authorize independent
interpretation, diagnostic conclusions or an independent report.

IMPLEMENTATION AND ROLLOUT
The shared policy drives the code reference, handbook, contract guide and newly
adopted payroll method. Authorize components through active staff-service
assignments after verifying the signed role. The latest applicable executed
agreement controls; a later removal of Testing cannot resurrect an older grant.
Runtime checks cover assignment writes, booking, service changes, documentation,
claim preparation and claim review. No new User.role enum or automatic grants.
New Testing medical catalog rows remain inactive until their payer pathway and
claim-unit setup are reviewed. Saved agency activation, overrides and billing
unit settings are preserved. New defaults do not reprice existing signed terms.

New-schedule payroll requires actual component minutes separately from claim
units, including dated import records. Do not allocate aggregate work across
dates by billed-unit ratios. Missing time requires reconciliation before pay
calculation. Extra-indirect submissions use actual Direct Care minutes, not
claim units, for the prorated allowance. Retain source work and approval records.
Inner Strength's individually negotiated schedules require separate payroll
setup; no other agency's matrix is assumed.

Reimbursement is a planning input, never the employee's pay rate. The old CCHA
Masters figures supplied for this review are historical comparisons only.
Current contracted allowed amounts, actual collections, supervision, test
licenses/materials, taxes, benefits and overhead remain separate inputs.

No migration or live activation is part of this change. Preserve the deployment
hold, then reconcile the branch against current main and the ongoing NLU UI /
Enrollment branch. Keep bookings 290/291, packages 76/77, existing signed records,
guardian permissions and existing cancellation terms intact. Do not transfer an
old signature to new text. Complete browser tests before release.
