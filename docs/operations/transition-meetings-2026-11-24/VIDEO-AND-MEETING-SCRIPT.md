## Example video update

Record short chapters so someone missing all meetings can find the exact task:

1. **Start here / dates (2 minutes):** notes due Sunday November 29 at 10 p.m.; access change Monday November 30 midnight–5 a.m.; contact the transition team for blockers.
2. **Sign-in and recovery (3 minutes):** where to set password, six-digit code and personal recovery email; demonstrate the reset path without revealing any code or actual recovery link.
3. **Client/intake review (4 minutes):** assigned chart, current intake, consents, authorized guardians and communication restrictions.
4. **Treatment plan (4 minutes):** current plan, dates, baselines, save, signatures and supervisor routing.
5. **Appointments and notes (6 minutes):** correct visit, saved/reloaded note, correction and signature workflow; what an unsigned/supervised note still needs.
6. **Next week's schedule (5 minutes):** verify every visit, recurrence exceptions, closures, location and join link; distinguish one visit from a series.
7. **Messages and email (4 minutes):** choose recipients, send/reply, secure attachments and task/notification follow-up.
8. **Finish the handoff (3 minutes):** records/schedule verification, approved cancellation of duplicate future TherapyNotes visits; optional “Transitioned to AuricWell” wording; keep historical records.
9. **Readiness/help (2 minutes):** self-check and contact the transition team with any unfinished step. Watching the recording alone does not establish readiness or recorded meeting attendance.

## Host checklist for each meeting

- Confirm each invited provider's client and next-week-session reconciliation before the meeting. Assign unresolved work; do not report it complete from invitation status.
- Confirm the video link, cohost permissions, Yes/No state, private RSVP URLs and correct CC/Reply-To.
- Keep the individual transition checklists open. Let providers demonstrate their workflows and record blockers separately from attendance.
- Record actual join/leave time, including breaks and rejoins. If attendance is in-person or by phone, capture authorized evidence rather than assume scheduled hours.
- After the meeting, finalize the full attendance register, including explicit absence. Only then release makeups, Rachel's missed/unselected report and attendance-based claim instructions.
- At the fourth meeting's completion, release the final no-attendance/Sunday follow-up. Sunday remains in-person only at the verified Windchime address.
