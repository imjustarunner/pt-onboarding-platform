Your family’s transition checklist

Staff: insert the family’s verified transition date, app address, next appointment, approved support contact/hours, urgent-contact instructions and verified agency texting numbers before sharing. Send through an authorized existing contact channel.

  • Confirm the correct parent, guardian or adult-client recipient and the best language/contact method.
  • Open your own invitation and verify the correct child or account. Ask support for help if it shows the wrong person or if you cannot access it.
  • Check the next appointment date, time, location and meeting link. Test your device before a virtual appointment.
  • If you choose texting, read the agency’s SMS disclosure and text START from the phone that will receive messages to the verified enrollment number. An account is not required to retain your phone consent; staff will verify the correct relationship before linking it.
  • Keep the verified support and provider numbers. Reply STOP to stop texts or HELP for help. A text opt-in does not give anyone access to your account.
  • Tell support if you do not want texts or cannot use the portal so an assisted alternative is arranged.

Sharing and privacy

Review the named recipients before using a shared portal message or session comment. Other currently eligible family users may see that conversation. Ordinary email is a separate channel; use the approved secure workflow for clinical information.
