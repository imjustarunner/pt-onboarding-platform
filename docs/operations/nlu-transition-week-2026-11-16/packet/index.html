Use the app for durable tracking

After the paired software release, open Management → AuricWell → EHR Transition in the selected practice. The full rollout view has Next Level Up, ITSCO and Inner Strength practice navigation. All workstreams are shown by default. Each board keeps assignments, evidence, unknown denominators, failed/deferred actions and training-video production with creator, due date, reviewer, watch link and publication timestamp. ITSCO ranking requires all active provider counts, freezes groups of five, and gates later groups on ordered acceptance and a monitored workday. The board does not itself retire accounts or switch financial routes.

Before any closure

  • All source charts, including inactive/discharged/unassigned clients, are accounted for and independently verified.
  • Original exports, app downloads, restricted Drive copies and database/document restoration are verified.
  • Families and providers have a working appointment/contact/access path.
  • Remaining notes, claims, ERA monitoring, balances, disputes and refunds have a verified handling path.
  • Clinical, records, billing and technology leads supply evidence; the transition lead authorizes closure.
  • Authorized account owner records cancellation confirmation and final billing date.

TherapyNotes storage mode disables claims, ERA, reminders and the portal. It cannot replace an active billing handoff. Monitor Grasshopper inbound for at least 14 days after a group’s handoff; voice/fax/number ownership need separate review.

Cadence and incidents

Assign owners/backups Monday. Protect work blocks. Hold morning, midday and afternoon reviews; the calendar file proposes times for schedule review. A privacy exposure, missing record, wrong-client action, duplicate financial transaction or unusable care workflow stops the affected rollout. Preserve evidence, name the temporary working system, and reconcile before resuming.
