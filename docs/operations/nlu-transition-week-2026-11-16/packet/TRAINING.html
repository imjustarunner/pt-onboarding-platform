Operations practice

  • Records: export a representative chart, preserve original metadata, match its existing ID, upload/download all volumes, reconcile the manifest and restore a restricted backup.
  • Family support: verify authority, confirm next appointments, use an assisted alternative, review an unmatched START without guessing a child, and stop duplicate outreach.
  • Billing: inventory a payer route, identify the legal billing entity, distinguish ERA from claims/EFT, match a controlled remittance, and hold unmatched or duplicate data.
  • Technology: demonstrate non-Google login/recovery, app mail after account suspension, revoked access, private downloads, actual phone delivery and STOP across senders.

Trainer record

Participant · own account/agency · scenario · actual result · evidence · unresolved issue · responsible owner · next practice date. Preserve prior learning progress and original records.
