# NLU combined October–November 2026 provider update

The November release includes the undelivered October material and the current NLU
handbook review changes. This release corrects the existing unsent NLU administrative
draft (agency 6, update 2) and its draft provider pushes, including private previews.

## Recipient experience

- The administrative update and provider-push title identify October and November 2026.
- School assignments, client follow-up and Spanish intake are disabled for this packet.
- Supervision announcements describe role-appropriate consultation and oversight. Only
  NLU staff actually marked prelicensed receive the licensure-hour review section.
- Services & Tutoring supports counseling, tutoring or both. A tutor must supply subjects,
  ages and grades; descriptive profile information is prefilled when available. Selection
  reuses existing qualification checks and does not enable online booking or change pay.
  Jeannie and Kimi can review/select both tutoring and their approved bachelor's-level
  counseling service. Their actual saved choices must be confirmed through their own
  update or the existing authorized agency service editor.
- Quick View is described as temporary until the new app, likely initially through
  AuricWell, brings information and notifications together.
- NLU's Security step requires a permanent app password, an unlocked six-digit code,
  a valid personal recovery address, and explicit safe-storage/access confirmations.
  Google deferral cannot complete this step. Actual credential changes still require
  the person's verified account session; no invitation-only password reset is added.
- Contact & Address precedes security preparation. The agency login link and
  `login?recovery=password` open ordinary sign-in/recovery. Existing SSO and recovery
  policy remains enforced until the authorized cutover.
- Routine personal-email reminders are described as temporary until app notifications
  are ready. The personal recovery address remains on file. This release does not
  prematurely disable either notification delivery or current Google access.
- Handbook Updates combines all current source review changes with saved local items,
  removes identical repetitions, and links the agency's own handbook. Distinct subjects
  sharing a policy section remain visible. Published handbooks and older digests remain
  intact; no handbook publication or acknowledgment is manufactured.
- Individual compensation amendments are enabled. The editor preserves compensation
  mode instead of converting it to a duties-only acknowledgment. Approved bachelor
  Direct Care and tutoring can both appear with their separate rates without implying
  psychotherapy duties. Existing signed documents remain unchanged.

## Amendments still require actual review and release

Use Provider Update → Compensation drafts to verify each person's approved service
roles, category, level, individual rates, existing agreement, prospective effective
date and countersigner. Release the reviewed document to the ordinary employee push.
Private previews are read-only. An unavailable or unsigned required amendment blocks
completion; enabling the section does not create agreed terms or a signature.

## Deployment and evidence

Migration `1835_nlu_combined_provider_update.sql` is generated by
`backend/src/scripts/buildNluNovemberProviderUpdateMigration.js`. It records original
content/configuration in `provider_update_content_revisions` with the explicit
`migration:1835_nlu_combined_provider_update` automation actor. It leaves issued pushes,
real invitations, original human audit actors, credentials, payroll and signatures
alone. Existing training configuration and media are preserved. Media-bearing prose
requires a manual merge rather than dropping a recording.

Backend startup checks successful migration logging and the completed content-revision
record. Its `[startup:nlu-provider-update]` line reports whether the draft changed or
was held because it was already issued. Use that result before claiming a live draft
was refreshed. Roll back paired application traffic if necessary; preserve revision
snapshots, original digests, signatures and any newly collected recipient work.

Validation before integration: 151 targeted backend tests (including four native MySQL
draft-preservation/reapplication checks), 80 frontend tests, and 20 compensation tests
passed. All 56 applicable source review entries are represented in the combined view
(48 distinct entries after identical repetitions are consolidated); the retired Spanish
intake item is omitted from this packet while its original policy/history is preserved. The private source
files used for testing matched the committed private-library manifest checksums.

The broader legacy autumn handbook test has four pre-existing failures involving
retired category assumptions and reconciled travel sections. They reproduce on the
unchanged base revision `0a9233397600ac055469fbc6ba7247a5d36db44f`; this release does not
rewrite those obsolete tests or restore the old compensation policy.
