# Personal phone access to staff meetings

The recovered source includes a dormant Vonage Voice-to-Video gateway for supervision, individual staff meetings, team/admin meetings, training, and huddles. Each invited account receives its own ten-digit PIN for each scheduled occurrence. This implementation connects to the app's existing Vonage rooms. External Google Meet rooms and clinical appointments are outside this gateway.

The scheduled-meeting and live-meeting screens include **Join or invite by phone**. Hosts can select an invited account and copy, text, or email its personal invitation. Attendees can retrieve or send their own invitation. Automatic invitation/reminder emails put phone details below the browser join link. Shared calendar entries never contain a personal PIN. Texting uses the agency's existing workforce sender, recipient consent, and delivery policies; unsuccessful or held delivery is shown accurately. The phone link supplies a pause and PIN where the dialer supports it; the number and PIN remain visible for manual entry.

Every telephone participant has a separate SIP bridge and separate Audio Connector stream. Four browser attendees plus a telephone attendee, two telephone attendees, and browser/phone changes during a session use the same persisted media room. The PIN establishes the account used for attendance and the speaker label. Forwarding a personal PIN lets its holder act as that participant, so invitations explicitly say to share only with the named person. One PIN cannot be active on two telephone calls simultaneously.

Telephone attendance starts when the SIP bridge answers, after waiting-room admission. Carrier callbacks and reconciliation close each caller's interval. Browser/phone overlap counts once; disconnect/rejoin gaps remain excluded. Existing scheduled-start, eligibility, rate, and approved correction rules continue to govern compensation. The implementation does not award everyone the longest caller's duration. Phone presence and browser presence use separate identities so browser heartbeats cannot erase a telephone connection or manufacture payable browser segments.

When phone access is configured, supervision browser, phone, and admitted calendar guests reserve places under the same session lock. A guest remains a separate visitor, never an account inferred from a display name. Unused browser connection tokens expire at a fixed database-backed deadline within the short reservation window; choosing **Rejoin** requests a fresh token and rechecks current access. Expiry does not disconnect an already connected SDK client. A connecting or connected carrier bridge retains its place until carrier/media disconnection is confirmed, including when a callback or cleanup attempt fails. A stale host or presenter role requires refreshing before browser entry. Phone access being off preserves the existing browser-only path.

Hosts joining by telephone hear a keypad choice after entering their PIN: **1** admits invited telephone participants automatically while that host remains connected; **2** keeps admission under the app's controls. An app host can also admit or remove callers in the phone panel. Hanging up leaves the meeting; **End for everyone** remains an app action. Removed phone access, roster removal, cancellation, rescheduling, meeting closure, or inactive membership prevents further access. The worker checks these conditions during an ongoing call and retries carrier disconnections. Unused PINs also stop working eight hours after the scheduled start, or at the scheduled end if later. Joining opens fifteen minutes before the scheduled start.

Supervision transcription starts by default when its existing transcription permissions/storage are ready, unless disabled, paused, stopped, or finishing. General meetings retain their existing manual transcription start. Each caller hears a transcription announcement and their audio enters the existing encrypted transcript/summary workflow under `Name · Phone`, including when everyone uses a telephone. Pause/stop revisions invalidate in-flight audio. A final pre-end segment can drain for up to two minutes, with samples after closure discarded and the summary queued again. The panel reports when telephone transcription is unavailable. The app cannot infer multiple people sharing one speakerphone from a single account PIN.

## Activation

The feature is disabled until explicitly configured. No numbers were purchased, existing Voice webhooks changed, messages sent, production migrations applied, or live calls placed during implementation.

The November 4 read-only configuration audit found all required columns in the three existing `meeting_dial_in_*` tables, but no dedicated meeting Voice application/number/origin/PIN configuration or Voice signature secret. Generic office Voice credentials do not enable this feature. The authorized host sees a configuration-needed message instead of a nonworking phone invitation. Existing office-call routes remain intact.

Provisioning, number assignment, enabling the feature, and live pilot calls require a separately reviewed configuration step. None were performed by this source recovery.

1. Deploy guarded fresh-install migration `database/migrations/1753_meeting_personal_dial_in.sql` (existing tables are left unchanged), the backend, and the frontend. Keep `MEETING_DIAL_IN_ENABLED=false` during setup.
2. Create/use a dedicated Voice application in the same Vonage account as Video. Enable **signed callbacks** and set its answer webhook to `POST https://<backend>/api/meeting-dial-in/voice/answer` and event webhook to `POST https://<backend>/api/meeting-dial-in/voice/events`. The returned NCCO supplies the PIN and host-choice POST callback URLs. Do not redirect the existing office Voice application's webhooks.
3. Link a Voice-capable number to that meeting Voice application. Configure `MEETING_DIAL_IN_NUMBER` in E.164 form, such as `+12025550123` (example only). Confirm SIP Connect to the number and Video SIP interconnect are available in the account. The bridge currently uses the US `sip-us.vonage.com` endpoint with TLS and secure media.
4. Configure `MEETING_DIAL_IN_VOICE_APPLICATION_ID` and its private key via `MEETING_DIAL_IN_VOICE_PRIVATE_KEY` or `MEETING_DIAL_IN_VOICE_PRIVATE_KEY_PATH`. The existing `VONAGE_APPLICATION_ID` and Video key continue to own Video sessions. Both dedicated values are required; there is no fallback to the office Voice application.
5. Set `VONAGE_VOICE_SIGNATURE_SECRET` to the Voice webhook signature secret; it is not the API secret or the Video monitoring secret. Set `MEETING_DIAL_IN_PIN_SECRET` to a stable random secret of at least 32 characters and retain the existing chat encryption key. Changing the PIN secret invalidates existing PIN lookups.
6. Set `MEETING_DIAL_IN_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` to the public HTTPS backend origin, without credentials, a path, query string, or fragment. Allow WebSocket upgrades at `/api/meeting-dial-in/audio`. Both the development listener and Cloud Run bootstrap attach this endpoint. Ensure background worker CPU is available, at least one backend instance remains running, and request timeouts support long-lived WebSockets. After a connection timeout, capture can restart on reconciliation; this needs a live deployment test.
7. Preserve the existing approved transcription provider/storage settings. Enable `MEETING_DIAL_IN_ENABLED=true` only after the callbacks and number are ready. New invitations/reminders then include PINs; existing invitees can retrieve them from the meeting screen.
8. Pilot with synthetic conversation: one browser plus one phone, two phones with host option 1, four browsers plus a phone, a switch between phone/browser, pause/resume, reconnect, a wrong/old PIN, removal, and ending while someone joins. Check actual audio in both directions, named transcript, durations and draft claims, final transcript drain, and inability to re-enter after closure. Carrier delivery, audio quality, DTMF automation and real billing require this live pilot.

To disable: set `MEETING_DIAL_IN_ENABLED=false`. With dedicated assets retained, the worker continues closing existing telephone calls; keep the deployment/credentials available until disconnection retries finish. Historical phone intervals remain part of attendance totals. If dedicated assets are absent, phone entry points and reconciliation perform no invitation/call writes or carrier operations. Do not remove the tables to disable the feature.

Usage fees still apply for the number, Voice/PSTN and SIP legs, Video SIP interconnect, Audio Connector, messages, and speech recognition as applicable to the account's contract. This feature does not require purchasing a HIPAA add-on for the non-HIPAA staff meeting scope described here. Review current account rates before the live pilot: [Vonage phone-to-video bridging](https://developer.vonage.com/en/use-cases/bridging-phone-calls-into-video-meetings), [SIP interconnect](https://developer.vonage.com/en/video/guides/sip-interconnect), [Audio Connector](https://developer.vonage.com/en/video/guides/audio-connector).
