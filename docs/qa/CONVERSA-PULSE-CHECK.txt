Conversa Pulse — team message analytics

Apply migration 1756_conversa_message_views.sql before releasing the backend
and frontend. No historical views are inferred or backfilled.

Use a team channel with a sender and at least two other current participants.
Repeat with a direct conversation, group, and a reply in a thread.

- Every participant can use the message's Pulse action, including a provider
  who did not send it. Hover or keyboard focus previews recorded viewers.
- Clicking Pulse opens Conversa Pulse. Check seen/recipient totals, people
  without a recorded view, replies, and reaction totals. Check filters and
  Refresh. The sender is excluded from the recipient denominator.
- Leave a message visible in a focused Team chat for at least one second.
  Refresh Pulse as another member; the viewer's name and first-view time appear.
- Leave another message outside the viewport. It must not gain a recorded view.
  Background tabs, unfocused windows, and content covered by a modal must not
  gain views. Returning focus after a modal closes must resume tracking.
- Sending, hiding, marking a thread read, and marking it unread must not invent
  or remove message views. Repeated visibility must retain the first-view time.
- Once someone views a message, the sender cannot unsend it even if the thread's
  unread cursor has not advanced.
- Counts refer to current conversation members. After membership changes, the
  recipient list must match that roster. A removed member cannot fetch Pulse.
- An outsider must receive access denied from the Pulse and views endpoints.
  A message ID from another thread must not expose stats or record a view.
  A message hidden for the requester must not expose its Pulse.
- Secure care conversations retain their existing activity log; Pulse endpoints
  deny access and the team Pulse action is absent.
- Network failures show a retry state, without fabricated zero stats. Switching
  messages must not display stale recipients from the previous message.
- Escape and backdrop click close the modal. Tab stays inside, focus returns
  to Pulse after closing, and the layout fits a narrow phone screen.

Do not interpret "No view recorded" as proof that someone never read a message.
Views made before rollout or from other chat surfaces may not be recorded.
