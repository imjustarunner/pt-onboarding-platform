PLOTLINE OFFER AND PRE-HIRE GROUP NOTICES
Release handoff and scoped live verification

Deploy backend with migration 1885_hiring_milestone_group_notices.sql first.
The existing hiring notification worker discovers and dispatches milestones.
No frontend deployment or new worker schedule is required.

Company prerequisites
- Enable and review People Operations submission notices in the existing hiring
  notification settings. Retain the approved reviewer and sender configuration.
- Select a verified People Operations sender identity. The PO destination is
  that identity's mailbox. The admin destination is the active tenant admin@
  identity on the same domain, using the existing applicant-admin resolver.
- Both mailbox identities must be configured. Missing or changed configuration
  holds the notice with a review reason. Ordinary email approval and demo gates
  continue to apply. No new mailbox is provisioned by this change.

Expected behavior
- A candidate's saved signature on an employment contract queues a congratulatory
  Offer signed notice to PO and admin, each with a Review candidate button.
- Completion of required pre-hire and submission queues a separate congratulatory
  Pre-hire completed notice. It states that the candidate signed their contract
  and completed pre-hire, and that the materials are ready for staff review.
- An offer signature alone never claims that unfinished pre-hire is complete.
- Use generated contracts or employmentContract-tagged library contract tasks.
  Acknowledgments, provider-update amendments, wrong signers and unsigned tasks
  do not produce these milestones. Required countersignatures retain their own
  status. These notices describe the candidate's signature and phase submission.
- Company name, logo and Plotline branding use the shared email frame with
  A People Operations Platform. Contract text and attachments are excluded.
- Only milestones after rollout and notification review are discovered. Already
  signed historical contracts are not mailed. Repeated discovery is idempotent.
- Failed/uncertain sends remain held for review. They are never blindly resent.
  Communication records and hiring_milestone_group_notices retain delivery state.

Coordinator live QA
1. In an approved synthetic company, verify PO/admin recipients and approved
   sender settings before triggering a new signature. Capture the build and IDs.
2. As the fictional candidate, sign one new offer through the ordinary portal.
   Leave a separate required pre-hire item incomplete.
3. Wait for the normal hiring worker. Confirm two workflow messages, one to each
   logical group. With QA redirection, inspect testing@itsco.health and both
   logical recipient records. Confirm signature wording, company/Plotline logos,
   quick-open link, sender/Reply-To and actual receipt. Transport acceptance is
   separate from inbox delivery evidence.
4. Open Review candidate as an authorized staff member. Confirm exact company
   and candidate. Signed-out users must authenticate and unauthorized accounts
   must remain denied by the existing hiring permissions.
5. Complete and submit the remaining pre-hire items normally. Confirm the later
   completion notice to each group and the truthful reviewed state in the app.
6. Reload/revisit, run another normal worker tick and verify no duplicate sends.
   Check an unchanged old signed offer and a pending/unsigned contract as negatives.
7. Record PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED with candidate, task, event and communication IDs,
   actual email receipts and screenshots. Preserve synthetic signed evidence.
