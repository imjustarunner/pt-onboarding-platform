NLU SESSION HUB V2 — ENGINEERING HANDOFF / NOVEMBER 21, 2026

Implemented scope
The dedicated Session Hub uses the same appointment-linked lesson for virtual and in-person tutoring. A compact participant panel, central board/worksheet, contextual learning/materials/notes/chat panel, focus mode and persistent session controls follow the supplied NLU mockups. The real booked room remains the media transport; preparation does not create attendance.

The board retains server-enforced tutor clear/lock authority, learner-owned ink and the append-only lesson ledger. Question/worked-example cards can move and resize in both dimensions. Native document fields support text, numeric/fraction responses, choice and pointer/pen ink. PDF/image sources, approved versions, exact attempts, revisions and reviews are retained. Learner payloads exclude tutor keys and private feedback. Actual tablet/Pencil usability still requires physical-device testing.

AI produces private reviewable questions and document field drafts. Text extraction and scanned-image/PDF OCR have an actionable manual-authoring fallback. Tutor review is required before sharing. Current approved plan/goal references, consistent four-level ratings and independence feed explicit progress recommendations. New evidence never silently rewrites a signed plan or declares mastery. Signed session activity records include performed work and worksheet version/attempt references.

The resource library separates tutor-private drafts, student-specific material and staff-reviewed general publication. Import makes a private copy for the exact session. Publishing does not share it with a learner automatically.

New native invitation links use an exact-booking, exact-recipient capability. The URL secret remains in the fragment and exchanges for a narrow HttpOnly guest proof, never an application JWT or whole-portal login. Email, eligible SMS and authenticated portal joins use the same invitation mechanism in enabled organizations. SMS consent and transport readiness remain required. Booking changes, removed guardian rights, explicit revocation and expiration are rechecked; active guest media access is disconnected. Guests cannot prepare a tutor workspace, admit people, sign notes or inspect private learning context.

NR21 repairs preserve makeup client/subject/origin and choose a future default; check student overlaps as well as provider conflicts; include eligible RSVP actions in real tutoring booking/change notices; maintain a waiting tutor's valid room without the alone timeout; clarify focus position/count; and avoid the application-wide loading overlay for Hub requests.
