Client assignments and profile experience — 2026-11-15

Counseling is one active service per client and agency. Program membership and
provider assignments are details of that service. Existing source IDs and
historical records are retained; separate agencies and different services remain
separate. An assigned service no longer displays a false missing-provider step.

Provider searches are client/agency scoped and keyboard accessible. Shared
physical offices appear in defaults, and chart/agency defaults stay synchronized.
Client and staff profiles show clearer section navigation, contextual icon
shortcuts, consistent form typography, and Agency terminology. School charts use
the same demographics workflow and retain education data during updates.

The release retains existing service-status confirmation, cancellation and audit
checks, re-enrollment/return workflows, provider-only access restrictions, portal
document assignment, secure composition, and paper-packet processing. Profile
compensation choices retain agency-defined categories and saved selections.
