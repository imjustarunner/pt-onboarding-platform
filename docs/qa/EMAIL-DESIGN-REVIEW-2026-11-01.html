ITSCO email design review

One review ID per sample/meaningful variant. The emails keep their actual template content and add the requested sender, audience, purpose and reply-route box. They are examples with fictional values, not evidence of an automated workflow or timed reminder.

Delivery: testing@itsco.health only. Billing samples use billing@itsco.health; school correspondence uses schools@itsco.health. AuricWell and SchoolCareBridge retain their product identity. Review replies return to the QA inbox. Sample action links open sign-in; no enrollment, payment, cancellation or signature is executed. Some supervision examples include a fictional publish-only calendar file.

These are source-render previews; the delivered email may add the app’s normal department signature and compliance footer. Stored fallback designs are labeled in the catalog and can be superseded by a workflow-specific renderer. Nine earlier sender copies are replaced by the current IDs.
