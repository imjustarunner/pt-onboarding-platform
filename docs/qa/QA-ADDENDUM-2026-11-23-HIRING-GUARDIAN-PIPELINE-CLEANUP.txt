CAUSE AND EXPECTED BEHAVIOR
- PENDING_SETUP is used by ordinary guardian/client account creation as well as
  hiring. The pipeline previously accepted that status without hiring evidence.
- Status alone now qualifies nobody. A row needs a scoped hiring profile, an
  explicit hire journey for this company, or a workforce role plus hired_at and
  unambiguous legacy company membership. Clinical enrollment remains unchanged.
- School affiliations do not count as another hiring company for that legacy
  check. A job in another company is not evidence of an application here.
- Jobless profiles use their explicit company journey or sole-company ownership.
  Ambiguous multi-company legacy rows require ownership review; no data is erased.
- Guardian-only/client-only contacts disappear from hiring views/counts after
  rollout and refresh. Their accounts, clinical links, permissions, status,
  documents and history are preserved. No deletion/archive/role rewrite is needed.
- A guardian who genuinely applies remains an applicant. A family setup status
  must not misclassify that application as pre-hire, and clinical documents must
  not contribute to that person's HR document/signature totals.
- Completed employees and not-hired applicants do not remain In Progress merely
  because they retain a hiring profile. Actual reapplications remain visible.
- The Applications filters, pre-hire/onboarding eligibility and direct candidate
  lookups use the same evidence boundary. Existing pre-hire/onboarding family-role
  restrictions remain in place; this patch does not convert a family account into
  an employee account or bypass the ordinary employee setup requirements.
