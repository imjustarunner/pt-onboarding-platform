Agency permissions transition — November 6, 2026

Employee profile > Account > Agency roles & permissions selects one company.
Only its administrators or a platform superadmin can change working roles and
permissions. Explicit company On/Off overrides selected platform defaults;
otherwise the preserved pre-transition company settings remain the fallback.
Superadmin authority remains platform-wide and cannot be granted by this card.

In the PlotTwistCo company card, superadmins can choose Platform defaults for an
active PlotTwistCo employee. These apply across all active platform companies,
including future companies, without creating employment or clinical affiliations.
Company overrides remain in place. Removing/deactivating the PlotTwistCo
affiliation suspends inherited permissions and selected platform service grants.
Reactivation restores the saved selections and retains company overrides.

Existing user roles, global permission flags, agency memberships, membership
roles/finance flags, and legacy tenant-expanded credentialing coverage are
snapshotted. Existing grants are not converted into opted-in platform defaults.
Managed billing relationships remain financial access only. Preserved tenant
grants are labeled separately and do not create employment affiliations.
New credentialing grants stay within the selected company.

Rollout procedure:
1. Migration 1771 creates the profile/default/audit tables with rollout disabled.
2. Deploy the matching backend and frontend and verify their build revisions.
3. From an environment configured for the intended database, run:
   node src/scripts/activateAgencyAccess.js --audit-dir=/absolute/new/audit/path
   This first invocation is read-only. Keep the resulting staff access snapshot.
4. Activate with a different, new audit directory:
   node src/scripts/activateAgencyAccess.js --apply \
     --expected-database=onboarding_stage --audit-dir=/absolute/new/cutover/path
   The technical migration actor is 0; optionally specify a verified active
   superadmin with --actor-user-id=ID. Do not infer a person's identity.
5. The transaction locks legacy source rows, reconciles every membership and
   permission against the latest state, retains before/after audit records,
   and enables company access only if no mismatches are found. This accounts
   for legacy edits between deployment and activation. Repeated activation is
   a no-op. Never overwrite an audit directory or bypass a reconciliation error.

Retain cutover-before.json and cutover-result.json in a protected local audit
directory; they contain staff names and access settings, not patient records.
Do not commit them. Do not disable rollout after company edits: legacy releases
cannot represent company-specific hiring/clinical flags or platform defaults.
Use a forward fix or a reviewed restoration from the retained audit instead.

Validation covers isolated MySQL migration/cutover, last-minute legacy edits,
local Off/inherit, virtual access without affiliation, future-company inheritance,
administrator boundaries, optimistic revisions, default suspension/reactivation,
managed billing, credentialing coverage, and the company selector UI. No real
employee memberships are changed by the automated tests.
