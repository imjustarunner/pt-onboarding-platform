SCOPE AND BEHAVIOR
- The new meeting selector is specific to NLU tutoring/learning appointments.
  The server resolves the saved agency: NLU live slugs, or the retained QA
  agencies 453/454 with their synthetic tutoring markers. A caller-supplied
  brand does not enable it. NLU counseling and other agencies retain their
  existing system-generated Google Meet behavior.
- Meeting option is separate from the selected service and subject. Choosing
  Math, Reading or General does not pick a different transport. Pricing, pay
  placement, permissions and clinical authority are not changed by this work.
- Platform Session Hub remains the new NLU tutoring default. Google Meet is an
  explicit alternative using the existing system-generated, appointment-specific
  Meet implementation. Existing permitted external URLs remain available under
  their existing policy. This is not a new personal/permanent room flow.
- Format/policy loading does not erase NLU's selected meeting option. In-person
  and no-video bookings keep their existing behavior. Upcoming recurring Meet
  appointments use their own links; preparation reuses an already saved link.
- Authorized booking staff see Meet readiness. The private host destination in
  staff appointment responses remains limited to the assigned tutor. Family
  join delivery still checks current relationship/service permissions.
- NLU portal, email and SMS destinations use the selected meeting. A stale
  calendar Meet URL cannot replace an explicit native Session Hub selection.
  Native guest invitations are not minted for a Meet-selected appointment.
- A Meet-selected Session Hub shows the saved meeting button and the same
  appointment's notes. It does not simulate native waiting-room admission or
  create platform media tokens. Merely opening Google Meet is not attendance.
- The assigned tutor explicitly confirms student presence and records the start.
  The audit identifies tutor_attestation, rather than claiming verified Google
  media attendance. Server time and the appointment window apply. Concurrent
  start retries preserve one start/audit. Missing links, ended/canceled sessions,
  and retained platform media records cannot create a new external start.
- Finish session record uses the existing learning completion, settlement and
  note workflow. The external Google call must be closed separately. Drafts
  stay private and sharing still requires an attended, ended session and the
  tutor's own review/signature. Recorded attendance prevents transport edits.
- Workspace/configuration failures preserve the saved booking and show retry
  guidance. Retrying preparation is not a second booking or a native fallback.
