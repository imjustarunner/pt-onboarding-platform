PRIVACY AND ROLE BOUNDARIES

Use only your own app identity. Review the recipient and your authority before sharing a document or message. Nonclinical staff route clinical questions to an authorized clinician.

ASKING FOR HELP

When a record is missing or a deadline conflicts, ask People Operations through the intended message path. Do not change another person's work or mark prerequisites complete to clear a checklist.

ACCOUNT READINESS

Setting a password is a setup step. Employee access starts after the required onboarding and staff approval. Keep passwords and personal portal links in the QA vault.
