QA Grade 3 - the garden observation

At the class garden, Lee noticed the soil near a small tree was dry. Lee and Jo put a ring of fallen leaves around the tree. They left space around its trunk. Each afternoon they checked the soil. After several days, the covered soil stayed damp longer than the uncovered soil. The class decided to keep observing both spots.

1. What is the main idea?

2. Give two details that support your idea.

3. Why did the class observe both spots?

4. Which statement is an observation rather than a guess?

5. What question would you investigate next?
