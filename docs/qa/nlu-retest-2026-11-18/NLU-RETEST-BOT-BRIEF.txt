## Part 2. Owner requirements (each one is schedule-wide, for every service)

**R1. No all-caps "TUTORING" or "TELEHEALTH".** Never show "TUTORING" or "TELEHEALTH" in all caps. Virtual tutoring is never labeled "Telehealth"; use "Online" or "Virtual". Applies to every label, dialog, error, list and email. *(Fix 9)*

**R2. 30-minute sessions.** 30-minute tutoring sessions must be bookable. A 60-minute minimum is a bug unless a package sets the length. Never change an end time silently. *(Fix 11)*

**R3. No-show and late-cancel flow.** A no-show opens a correction window (default scheduled end + 10 minutes). A late cancel is a cancellation inside the agency's notice window (default 24 hours, set per service) and uses the same flow. The family email follows the draft in Part 3. The provider gets a copy. Fees or package sessions follow policy. *(Fix 1, 2, 3)*

**R4. Makeup that week.** While a makeup is linked, hold the fee. When the makeup is attended, waive the fee and audit the waiver. *(Fix 8)*

**R5. Move rules for missed sessions.** A missed session can move only within the same calendar week. There is an easy Reschedule action. Dragging a past missed session asks for confirmation. Moving beyond the week is blocked with a suggestion to keep it and book a new session. Untouched future sessions move freely and send moved-session emails. *(Fix 7)*

**R6. Locked sessions.** Cancelled, late-cancel and no-show sessions open nearly read-only with only a Reschedule button. A session with a signed note can't be moved. An ended session shouldn't be movable. *(Fix 6, 15, 16)*

**R7. Calendar visibility.** Cancelled, late-cancel and no-show sessions stay visible on the calendar. *(Fix 14)*

**R8. Attendance history.** A running record on the staff client profile and the parent portal, with correct statuses. Past sessions must not stay "scheduled". *(Fix 15)*

**R9. Tutoring notes.** Simple progress tracking, not clinical: three lines (worked on, how it went, practice at home), tied to goals and the subject track, with an attachment and a sign-and-share step. The signed summary shows in the parent portal (Recent session summary) with shared worksheets. Note times match the session. *(Fix 16)*

**R10. Session Hub (owner-chosen name).**
- Drop the standing virtual room. Each session opens its own Session Hub with that client's data loaded: learning plan, goals, documents, tools and resources, past notes, attendance, live progress tracking, and optional audio recording. If a standing room stays, it is only a lobby that opens the right session's hub.
- Two modes with the same tools and a mode label: **Virtual** (video) and **In person** (tutor iPad or desktop beside the student, no video).
- Tutor view (desktop and iPad): shared work area in the middle (whiteboard, worksheet viewer, screen share) with video as a small strip; left side shows only this student (today's plan, subject tracks, last note and practice, documents, saved resources); right side is live tracking (timer, quick skill checks, running note that becomes the tutoring note, chat). Top bar: student and subject, Live or Waiting indicator with one-tap Admit, recording toggle, End session. On iPad the sides collapse to tabs and the pencil works on the whiteboard.
- End session pre-fills the note from live tracking, signs in one step, and updates progress.
- Audio recording: optional, off by default, set per agency. Clear "Recording" indicator for everyone; family consent captured at enrolment and confirmed on join; recordings kept with the session; plain wording, no clinical warnings.
- Client view is phone-friendly and simple: tutor name, subject, time, "Test your camera and mic", one big Join; full-width video; bottom bar with Mute, Camera, Chat, Leave; no tutor tools. After the session the short summary appears in the portal.
- Remove "AuricWell VIRTUAL OFFICE" from NLU rooms. Keep the per-appointment room address for virtual (`/tutoring-session/appointment/<id>`) and use the same pattern for in-person.
- Tests to add: Hub on desktop and iPad width shows only that student's plan, documents, tools and resources; live tracking; End prefills the note; recording toggle, indicator and consent; parent phone (390 px) and desktop views. *(Fix 17)*

**R11. Tutors never set prices or discounts.** Only authorized support, booking or admin staff may set a customer price, with a reason. Waivers are policy actions. No warning banners and no clinical, HIPAA or beta wording on tutoring screens. *(Fix 10; Finley saw no price controls)*
