JOB QUESTIONS — POSTING SAVE AND INTERVIEW GUIDE RETEST
November 18, 2026

Engineering found two save defects:
- The job posting's main Save action omitted unsaved question-editor changes.
- Replacing an uploaded job-description PDF created a version without carrying
  the previous version's interview guide forward.

A legacy job without a saved default also opened a blank question editor.
An empty company template could yield no starter questions in the scheduler.

The main Save changes action now saves the posting and edited questions in one
transaction. A failure keeps the edits visible. Closing or navigating away
warns about unsaved questions. A PDF replacement receives its own copy of the
previous guide and rating criteria, or the explicitly edited questions. Failed
replacement rolls back and retains the original active posting.

Saved job questions load into a visible preview. Legacy jobs show clearly
labeled company/Plotline starter questions without silently writing a guide.
Empty company templates use the platform's five starter questions. The job
editor can copy questions from this job's other sets or company-shared sets.

The candidate interview scheduler starts with the linked job's default. Its
arrows/dropdown switch whole sets, and individual questions can be added,
removed and reordered. A new set can be private to this job or shared with all
jobs in this company; creating it does not change any job's default. Pending
default edits must be saved/canceled before scheduling. The candidate detail
shows questions saved for an already scheduled interview separately from the
job's current guide. Existing interview snapshots remain intact.

DATA REVIEW AND LIMITS
- Engineering inspected only job metadata, guide pointers and question counts
  through an existing operational connection in a read-only transaction.

  by engineering. No interviews, invitations or application identities were

- No database migration or mass backfill is needed. A displayed starter is not
