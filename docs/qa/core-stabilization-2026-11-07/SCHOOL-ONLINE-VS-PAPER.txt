SUGGESTIONS TO EVALUATE AFTER TESTING
1. Keep the approved paper copy as-is. Use a separate internal staff checklist
   for translating paper fields and ROI decisions into the electronic record.
2. Label online-only payment/card-upload steps clearly. A paper completion should
   never be held up by those online-only extras.
3. Treat changed/missing questionnaire items as a clinical-review question; do not
   silently add online questions to the paper packet or infer missing answers.
4. Verify electronic permissions reflect the family's actual paper ROI decisions.
   Uploading a signed PDF alone is not proof those permissions were recorded.
5. Keep language/version/school identifiers visible in the testing evidence so
   different saved templates are not mistaken for regressions.
