Company events audit and implementation — November 11, 2026

The old editor mixed company gatherings with service enrollment, guessed SMS status from absent feature flags, displayed expired groups and guardians as staff choices, hid reminders behind legacy voting, and silently cleared successful saves. The directory loaded only the newest 300 records before filtering, allowing future school calendars to bury company events and undated plans.

Implemented:
- Upcoming and planning view by default, searchable/sortable event directory, separate past/inactive/type filters, compact secondary-action menu.
- Five-step event editor with invitation preview and persistent congratulations. Company events have no required program affiliation; program registration remains available.
- Searchable/sortable staff and coordinator pickers with role/email/agency context; current active eligible staff only, no expired/inactive groups or demo/guardian accounts.
- Cross-agency invitation scope validated against the organizer's management access. Optional attendance by default, mandatory attendees explicitly selected from the effective audience.
- Date/time can be undecided. Event remains on a stable private invitation page with RSVP, current details, multiple modern linked votes, potluck and group conversation.
- Separate RSVP and event votes. Current parent invitation is enforced for linked-vote listing, delivery and submission, including cross-agency invitees and membership revocation.
- One event-named internal group direct message, accessible in Direct Messages and Groups. Conversation access follows current invitees; no channel is created. General group DM composer supports multiple staff recipients.
- Private invitee page by default; public registration is explicit and explained. Payroll controls and check-in are limited to work events, preserving existing program workflows and open-punch checkout.
- Organizer potluck requests and invitees' own suggested contributions can be updated transactionally without duplicate claims.
- Independent configurable RSVP reminders defaulting to every 48 hours (email/in-app), stopping after any RSVP. Attendance reminders at seven days/24 hours for Yes/Maybe, skipping undated events.
- Multiple scheduled invitee-only dashboard announcements; removed/rescheduled/expired announcements and revoked invitees are rechecked before display.
- Authoritative workforce SMS readiness and Yes/No/Maybe RSVP replies, with existing sending permissions and ambiguous-event handling retained.
- Branded event invitation email from events@; multiple agencies use PlotTwistCo events@ and display both hosts. Personal reply links require confirmation before recording attendance preferences.
