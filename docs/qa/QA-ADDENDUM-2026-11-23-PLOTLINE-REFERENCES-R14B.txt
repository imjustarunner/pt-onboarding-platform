CHANGES TO RETEST AFTER RELEASE
- A decline is saved together with a durable staff-alert event. A queue failure
  rolls back the decline; the referee can retry instead of silently losing it.
- Completed and declined replies use the same permission-checked alert worker.
  SSO hiring staff receive only work email; non-SSO hiring staff receive only a
  personal app notification. Both identify the applicant/referee and open the
  exact applicant, reference and request. Revoked hiring access blocks delivery.
- Decline alerts say Reference unavailable and Unable to provide a reference.
  They never claim a questionnaire was completed or promise answers to review.
- The referee confirmation says Response recorded / Thank you for letting us
  know. Reload says the request was already marked unable to provide, with no
  questionnaire, employer/applicant details or second submission available.
- Staff-cancelled and expired links retain distinct unavailable/expired wording.
- Plotline navigation now includes Notifications and a company-scoped unread
  badge. The link opens this company's personal inbox. The badge refreshes on
  navigation/focus and at a one-minute interval, pauses during session lock and
  ignores responses from a previous company, actor or unmounted workspace.
- A decline does not send an applicant completion notice or referee thank-you
  email. Those messages still require a genuinely completed reference.
- Existing declined requests are not replayed/backfilled into the new queue.
  Do not resend Morgan or re-trigger its decline to obtain an alert screenshot.
