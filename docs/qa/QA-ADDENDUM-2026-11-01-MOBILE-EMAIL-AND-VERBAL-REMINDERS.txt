ADDITIONAL QA PLAN — NOVEMBER 1, 2026
Shared mobile email spacing and support-recorded verbal appointment-text consent

Applies to shared application workflows, not only to the demo tenant. Use the
existing scoped synthetic fixtures and controlled inbox/approved phones for QA.
This plan is not a claim of completed live delivery or consent from any person.

EMAIL LAYOUT
- Trigger one actual caseload email matching the reported phone screenshot.
  Inspect its real receipt on a phone. Header art uses the available column;
  the message has a single readable gutter rather than nested narrow padding.
- Check portal enrollment, school, billing, guardian/contact and secure-message
  variants using their current permitted workflow. Preserve sender, Reply-To,
  language, recipient, branding and action. Do not resend already received mail.
- At 320, 390 and 430 CSS pixels: no horizontal scrolling, clipped buttons or
  compressed brand/agency names. Clinical portal headers stack their two brands.
  Check long names/addresses, enlarged text, images blocked and desktop width.
- Signature details remain readable; the secondary brand moves below on phones.
  Footer links follow the art in normal flow and must not overlap it.
- Engineering rendered shared caseload, portal and contact examples at these
  phone widths and 800px desktop. Real Gmail/Outlook receipt remains a separate
  check. Existing already-sent emails will not change.

SUPPORT VERBAL-CONSENT WORKFLOW
- In a client's Overview, support/admin can open Appointment text reminders.
  No clinical-note access or role promotion is needed. School staff, providers,
  guardians and another agency's support account cannot use this staff action.
- Choose one eligible saved guardian/client phone, verify recipient identity,
  appointment authority and control of that exact phone. Adult clients may use
  their own saved contact phone without creating a portal account. A minor's
  unrelated/unverified contact must not be treated as an authorized guardian.
- Staff read the displayed disclosure, record phone call/in-person and actual
  consent time, and attest that the recipient verbally agreed. This creates a
  named staff attestation, never a substitute recipient signature. The entry
  window is seven days; older consent evidence needs review.
- For synthetic testing use an explicitly simulated conversation; do not claim
  any real parent gave consent. Do not manufacture consent in live family data.
- When the reminder program is ready, saving enables this recipient's appointment
  text preference and creates phone/client/recipient-bound permission evidence.
  Other guardians, email selections, billing, marketing and secure care-message
  permissions must not change. Shared-phone sibling grants remain distinct.
- Activation requires the agency's actual approved/linked Notifyre reminder
  sender and approval of verbal reminder script version2026-11-01.1. An existing
  agency administrator records carrier verification in Campaign registration
  and consent; support cannot declare carrier approval. Saving that flag is
  not a carrier submission. Do not check it simply to pass this test.
- If setup is unavailable, save the attestation as pending and explicitly show
  that texts remain off. Do not use a workforce sender as a substitute.
- After activation inspect the one subscription confirmation in actual receipt
  evidence. Accepted/queued is not delivered. A failed/uncertain confirmation
  requires delivery review; an HTTP retry must not send it a second time.
- STOP always blocks activation. Staff editing/attestation cannot clear STOP.
  Only the registered recipient restart flow can clear campaign suppression.
- Change/revoke the selected recipient or phone and test an old due reminder.
  Old verbal permission must not follow a replacement phone or another client.
  A newer recipient decline must not be overridden by older verbal evidence.
- Recheck actual due reminder and cancellation paths, including parent-specific
  choices. No phone reminder is sent simply because a checkbox was saved.
- Reconcile immutable SMS consent events and staff audit rows: actor, client,
  recipient, phone suffix, disclosure/version, time, activation and confirmation
  result. An audit-write failure must roll back consent/preference activation.
