Appendix · Employee access

Google Workspace sign-in for your agency

Agency-specific Google sign-in and required-SSO policies are supported for employees who already have an app account and agency membership. An administrator assigns the employee’s confirmed Workspace email to that account and configures the agency’s allowed domains and policy. The employee can then use that Google identity instead of an app password.

Someone present only in your Workspace directory is not automatically enrolled today. Just-in-time account creation is not implemented in the current Google sign-in callback; employees first need an app account and authorized agency membership. Domain ownership, identity-provider configuration, and access policy must be confirmed before requiring SSO. An email domain alone does not grant access, and a temporary demo profile does not establish an employee identity.
