## Appendix · employee Google Workspace sign-in

Agency-specific Google sign-in and required-SSO policies are supported for employees who already have an app account and membership in the agency. After an administrator assigns the employee’s actual Google Workspace email to the correct app account and configures the agency’s allowed domains and policy, the employee can use that Google identity instead of an app password.

A person who exists only in your Workspace directory is not automatically enrolled in the app today. Just-in-time account creation is not implemented in the existing Google sign-in callback. They first need an app account and authorized agency membership. Domain ownership, identity-provider configuration, employee membership, and access policy must be confirmed before requiring SSO. An email domain alone is not authorization. Temporary demo profiles do not establish employee identities or grant Google access.
