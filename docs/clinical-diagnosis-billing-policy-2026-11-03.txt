CLINICAL DIAGNOSIS CONTROLS AND SESSION ALERTS
Implementation reference — November 3, 2026

IMPLEMENTED BEHAVIOR
Every entered diagnosis requires its own provider-reviewed clinical justification.
The final note keeps the diagnoses in order and a separate rationale for each.
90791/H0031 generation asks for source-supported rationale for every diagnosis;
missing source facts and supporting records must not be invented. Import/review
screens preserve each diagnosis rationale instead of assigning one paragraph to
all diagnoses. Existing signed versions are not rewritten.

Primary diagnosis coverage uses the requested covered MH/SUD ranges. SDOH primary
codes require age under 21 and a verified eligible service, including the separate
assessment/screening exception. Other valid billable ICD-10-CM codes may be used
secondarily. Bachelor/QBHA providers cannot author or bill F diagnoses, including
secondary F diagnoses. An assigned licensed supervisor must explicitly authorize
non-F primary diagnoses for their supervisee; changes retain actor, reason, time.
H0002 screening retains its existing R69 exception and does not require an invented
mental-disorder diagnosis or block signed-note visibility.

Primary autism/ADHD requires reviewed supporting medical documents belonging to
that client/agency OR a qualified licensed clinician's own documented evaluation.
Lack of evidence creates a review blocker; it does not silently replace diagnoses.
The evidence panel preserves prior clinician identity/date when a later reviewer
records follow-up. Reviews and permissions append to history; free-text evidence
and reasons use clinical payload encryption. New checks run before initial note
signature, cosignature, exact-version attestation, intake finalization, claim
readiness and transmission. A clinically approved row remains visible in billing;
policy blockers are not permission to submit an unsupported claim.

Unspecified codes are identified from the official catalog, not merely a user-
entered description. After more than 90 days, they remain flagged while active,
even if a review has been recorded. Flags display in the clinical record, note
workspace, appointment detail, and schedule for authorized providers/supervisors.
Adjustment disorder requires an event month. At six months from that recorded
month, the organization's requested review/billing hold applies. This is a strict
organization rule, not a claim that all clinical adjustment-disorder diagnoses
universally expire six months after an event; ongoing stressors need clinical
review. The system does not manufacture a new event date or alternative diagnosis.

Appointment alerts remain on the appointment and its encrypted history, not on a
claim. Authorized billing, admin, assigned supervisor, or the scheduled provider
can create an alert. The note author must acknowledge the exact alert revision
before starting/continuing a draft or generation. New alert revisions require a
fresh acknowledgement; actor/revision/time are retained. Historical signed notes
remain readable. Diagnosis flags remain visible after acknowledgement.

VERIFIED PRIMARY SOURCES
1. HCPF Behavioral Health Policies, Standards, and Billing References:
   https://hcpf.colorado.gov/bh-policies
   Accessible indexed state mirror used for the diagnosis tables:
   https://hcpf.test.colorado.gov/bh-policies
   Covered MH: F20.0–F69; F90.0–F98.4; F98.8–F99;
   R45.1–R45.2; R45.5–R45.82. Covered SUD: F10.10–F19.99.
   Autism F84.0–F84.9 only for members under 21 and the published psychotherapy
   service-code list. Screening/assessment/crisis/prevention have documented
   exceptions; R69/Z03.89 may be appropriate when no diagnosis has been determined.

2. HCPF January 2026 State Behavioral Health Services Billing Manual:
   https://hcpf.colorado.gov/sites/hcpf/files/January%202026%20SBHS%20Billing%20Manual1.13.26.pdf
   Appendix I explains blue SDOH service highlights and yellow assessment/
   screening/crisis/prevention exceptions. Indexed text was readable; direct PDF
   image download was denied by the state server (403), so the service allowlist
   was independently checked against the explicit state lists below.

3. HCPF Services/Codes Included in SB23-174 Coverage Policy, revised March 1, 2024:
   https://hcpf.colorado.gov/sites/hcpf/files/Revised%20Services_Codes%20to%20Include%20in%20the%20SB23-174%20Coverage%20Policy_0.pdf
   Lists 18 services. Later official annual report Appendix A lists 20:
   https://hcpf.colorado.gov/sites/hcpf/files/HCPF%20174%20Annual%20Report.pdf
   Verified list used: 90785,90791,90832,90834,90837,90846,90847,90849,90853,
   90875,90876,H0004,H0005,H0038,H2014,H2017,H2023,H2027,S9445,T1017.
   H0002/H0031 assessment handling is a separate exception; they are not mislabeled
   as blue-highlighted treatment codes. Agency policy may restrict the verified
   SDOH service list through clinicalDiagnosisPolicy.verifiedSdohServiceCodes.

4. CMS official ICD-10-CM release page and billable tabular-order code files:
   https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/icd-10-codes
   https://www.cms.gov/files/zip/2026-code-descriptions-tabular-order.zip
   https://www.cms.gov/files/zip/april-1-2026-code-descriptions-tabular-order.zip
   https://www.cms.gov/files/zip/2027-code-descriptions-tabular-order.zip
   Server-only catalog includes billable leaf codes and unspecified classification
   for dates October 1, 2025 through September 30, 2027. File source URLs and SHA256
   hashes are retained in backend/src/data/icd10cm-diagnosis-catalog.json.
   Dates outside a verified edition require catalog verification before billing.
   F84.9 is "Pervasive developmental disorder, unspecified"; F90.0/F90.1/F90.2
   identify ADHD presentations, while F90.9 is unspecified. Those clinical meanings
   are not interchangeable merely because outside documentation is missing.

VALIDATION AND RELEASE REQUIREMENTS
Clinical migration028 stores evidence reviews and supervisor permission history.
Primary migration1737 stores appointment alert acknowledgements.
Both migrations were applied successfully to an isolated local synthetic MySQL
schema. Tests verify FK preservation, actor/revision acknowledgement, stale
rejection, re-review after alert edits, authorization, code-specific rationale,
evidence encryption/ownership, exact-version signing and claim blockers.
No live patient data was changed or used for these tests. Live acceptance still
requires deployed migrations and authenticated synthetic workflow testing.
