1. Platform license and services

Platform Provider grants Operator a nonexclusive, nontransferable right during the term to use the hosted platform to operate SchoolCareBridge and provision approved agency and school access. Services include the configured school portals, agency operations interface, program catalog, bookings, registration links, financial tracking, invoices, payment integration, maintenance and reasonable technical support. Operator supplies program operations, partner relationships, content, participant support and program administration. Clinical care and professional services remain the responsibility of the delivering agency or presenter. New modules, custom deliverables and service-level commitments require a written order describing scope, price and acceptance.

2. Launch and development period

Rollout begins 2027-03-31. The development-rate period lasts twelve months, through 2028-03-30, inclusive; the ongoing percentage applies the following day. The development share compensates actual platform development, configuration and launch support, rather than clinical referrals. No upfront development fee or retroactive pre-rollout platform fee is created by this agreement. Dates and rates can change only by a signed amendment. An earlier complimentary agreement between MH4Kidz and ITSCO remains a separate customer arrangement.

3. Standard usage value and fees

MH4Kidz share of program delivery fee20.00%

For each participating agency, an enabled, active affiliated school counts as one school portal; an active provider with an active assignment to at least one of that agency’s enabled schools counts as one provider slot; an active agency administrator counts as one administrator slot. Each provider counts once within an agency regardless of the number of schools served. Each school-agency affiliation counts separately. Platform super-administrator support accounts do not count. Deactivated accounts, archived schools and inactive affiliations do not count. Other users are excluded unless added by signed amendment.

Usage is measured in daily UTC snapshots, retained with identifiers supporting the counts. Standard monthly unit rates are prorated by counted days divided by calendar days in the billing month. Each agency/unit/percentage subtotal is rounded once to the nearest cent. A March 31 launch therefore accrues one day of March usage. Platform fee equals the applicable development or ongoing percentage multiplied by standard usage value, irrespective of the amount MH4Kidz actually collects. Missing usage evidence must be reconciled before an invoice is issued; estimates cannot silently replace missing days.

Free and discounted offers do not waive Operator’s obligation. From rollout onward, MH4Kidz may discount or waive its customer charge, but remains responsible for the contracted platform percentage of the standard scheduled value for every counted school, provider and administrator slot. Free trials, sponsorships, grants, waived collections, bad debt and promotional credits do not reduce that basis. Only a written amendment signed by Platform Provider expressly identifying a platform fee waiver can waive those fees. Operator should budget an unrestricted subsidy when it promises free access.

4. Program bookings and presenter compensation

Every booking requires a written quote specifying the delivery fee, presenter, scope, date/time/timezone, location or virtual format, capacity, language/accessibility arrangements, travel, school contribution, funding source, shares, cancellation and payment dates. The standard delivery fee is allocated to Platform Provider at the program platform percentage, to MH4Kidz at the program operator percentage, and the remainder to the presenter. Separately approved travel at cost is payable to the presenter and excluded from both percentage bases; taxes and processor charges are excluded from that basis. MH4Kidz bears processor costs unless the accepted quote expressly provides otherwise. A free or subsidized booking still requires an approved budget for presenter compensation and contracted platform fees. No party may deduct a platform share from a restricted grant unless the award and approved cost allocation allow that actual service expense.

MH4Kidz collects customer program payments as the program operator and is responsible for presenter payment after delivery and the agreed payment date. The app records those obligations as finance expenses; collection does not itself mean a presenter has been paid. No automatic payout or grant disbursement is authorized merely by publishing a program. Additional referral commissions, insurance reimbursements, charitable contributions and unrelated grants are outside this fee base.

5. Invoices, in-app payments and receipts

Platform Provider issues an itemized monthly invoice to MH4Kidz after the service month, showing counted usage, standard rates, percentage, period and credits. Program platform fees and presenter obligations are separately itemized against the booking. MH4Kidz invoices the school or purchaser only for its accepted cash contribution. Payment is due within 30 days of invoice issuance unless a booking order says otherwise. Payment is available through the authenticated app when the correct merchant account is enabled. A receipt is created only after payment is verified by the payment processor, with invoice number, paid amount, currency, date and payment reference. Drafts, pending payments and grant allocations are not receipts.

An invoice dispute must identify the disputed lines within 30 days of receipt; undisputed amounts remain due. Parties reconcile counts and processor records and issue a documented credit, corrected invoice or refund when appropriate; paid history is retained. No unilateral rate change, automatic paid conversion of a customer trial, or reallocation of existing agency platform charges is authorized. The parties will review overlapping services to avoid billing the same obligation twice.

6. Ownership, confidentiality and data

Each party retains its preexisting software, trademarks, curriculum and materials. Platform Provider retains its platform and general improvements; MH4Kidz retains its supplied program content, brand and business records. Presenters retain their preexisting materials unless a written order assigns them. Each party grants the other only the limited licenses needed to perform and display approved attribution. Neither party may sell confidential information, use participant records for unrelated advertising, or train general-purpose models on identifiable records without separate lawful authority. Access is limited to authorized personnel with a business need. A separate BAA controls protected health information. School education records require applicable school data-sharing authority; this contract grants no right to disclose them.

7. Nonprofit approval and conflicts

The parties must obtain all required organizational approvals before execution. If an officer, director, founder or other decision-maker has a financial interest in both parties or a presenter arrangement, that interest must be disclosed and the interested person must not approve their own compensation on behalf of MH4Kidz. MH4Kidz will document independent approval, comparable pricing and the basis for reasonable compensation. The rates are contractual proposals until approved and signed; no representation of fair market value or tax deductibility is made here. Grant restrictions prevail over an internal allocation. Unallowable grant costs remain MH4Kidz’s responsibility from eligible funds.

8. Performance, responsibility and liability

Each party will perform its services with reasonable care and comply with law applicable to its role. MH4Kidz is responsible for program descriptions, presenter qualification review, required consents, safeguarding procedures and lawful funding use. Platform Provider is responsible for platform operation and its subcontractors. Neither party guarantees clinical or educational outcomes. Each party is responsible for losses caused by its breach, negligence or unlawful acts to the extent permitted by law. Neither party assumes the other’s clinical liability. No limitation in a commercial order limits mandatory HIPAA duties, fraud, intentional misconduct, or payment of earned fees. The parties may negotiate insurance, liability caps and indemnity in a signed amendment; none are implied by the app.

9. Term, termination and transition

The agreement begins on the effective date and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate on 60 days’ written notice. A material breach may be terminated after written notice and 30 days to cure, or immediately where continued performance would be unlawful or threaten information security. Accrued fees remain payable; termination does not accelerate unearned future development fees. The parties will coordinate a 30-day export and transition period, subject to necessary security restrictions. Data return, lawful retention and destruction follow the BAA and applicable record-retention duties. Ending this agreement does not erase paid invoices, receipts or audit records.
