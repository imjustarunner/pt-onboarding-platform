Expected behavior
- Each guardian with current appointment access receives reminders using their own contact details and notification choices. For office/virtual sessions, both can respond Y/N/R or use their own email/portal response.
- A guardian cancellation changes the single shared appointment for everyone. The canceling guardian receives acknowledgment. The other guardian receives a cancellation notice attributed to another parent or guardian on the account. Provider cancellations identify the provider instead.
- Both cancellation texts include: For future sessions and information, please log in at https://auricwell.com.
- Dates/times use the appointment timezone. The other guardian's private explanation, enrollment answers, documents and access links are not copied into the notice.
- A parent's cancellation notice is eligible for the next worker run. It does not wait for the normal provider-edit quiet window. Sending still requires an enabled worker, current access and an authorized channel.
- Y or R after cancellation must not reopen the appointment. Repeated delivery processing must not resend a completed cancellation notice.
- A late cancellation with a fee remains scheduled until the applicable fee is reviewed and acknowledged. Do not claim that replying N alone charged or canceled a fee-bearing appointment.
- School reminders retain notice/absence reporting behavior; do not send Y/N/R cancellation prompts or apply an automatic cancellation fee to a school absence.
- Shared destinations are deduplicated. If the sending phone cannot identify a single guardian/dependent/appointment, the response directs the sender to sign in instead of guessing.
