QA ADDENDUM — ALERT THE OTHER PARENT WHEN A SHARED MESSAGE IS POSTED
October 27, 2026. Retain earlier instructions and evidence.

Expected behavior
- A message posted by Parent A in the dependent's shared secure conversation alerts Parent B and the other authorized participants. A message from Parent B alerts Parent A. A provider message alerts both authorized parents.
- Do not send the posting parent a new-message email about their own post.
- Guardian email wording: "A new message has been posted to your dependent's account. Please sign in to AuricWell to read this message and reply."
- The email contains a recipient-specific secure link. It contains no message body, attachment contents, child name or private explanation. Sign-in and current conversation access remain required.
- The text no longer says the agency sent the message when another parent posted it.
- Adult self accounts and staff receive neutral secure-conversation wording rather than being called a dependent's guardian.
- Refresh authorized membership before notifying. Revoked or inactive participants must not receive the notification.
- If one recipient's delivery fails, continue attempting the remaining recipients and record the failure for review. Do not blindly resend a message whose delivery result is uncertain.
- Preserve individual notifications and access links for different accounts even if they share an email address.
- Existing notification settings and email delivery controls remain in force. A queued/held notification is not proof of delivery.

Bot checks — office and school shared conversations
1. Sign in separately as both authorized guardians for one synthetic dependent. Record the displayed shared-thread participants.
2. Parent A posts a synthetic message. Verify B's notification subject, wording, recipient and secure link; A should not receive a self-notification.
3. Open B's link. Verify the correct login is required and the intended conversation opens after sign-in. Check that an unrelated account cannot read the thread.
4. Repeat with Parent B posting, then with the provider posting. Confirm the other parent, or both parents for the provider post, receive notifications.
5. Verify the body and any attachment appear only inside the authorized secure conversation. Inspect email source as well as the rendered email for accidental preview disclosure.
6. Verify each notification's recorded sent/held/failed status and actual inbox receipt separately. On test delivery failure for one recipient, verify the other still receives their notification.
7. Revoke a controlled fixture's messaging access and verify no later email is sent to that account. Restore only the fixture's original access afterward.
8. Check ordinary private documents and payer-specific details remain separate from the shared conversation.

For separated guardians, explicitly show that messages posted in this shared conversation are visible to both authorized parents and the care team. Do not represent it as a replacement for TalkingParents or assume permission from appointment access alone.
