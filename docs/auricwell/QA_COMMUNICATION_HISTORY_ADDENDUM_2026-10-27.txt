NEW BEHAVIOR
1. A recipient's first successfully sent guardian enrollment invitation uses
   "complete your guardian enrollment". A subsequent send is a reminder only
   when a previous invitation was successfully sent to the same address.
   A failed or suppressed attempt does not make the next email a reminder.
2. New explicit sends enroll in a limited follow-up schedule: after 2 days,
   then after 7 days from the initial send, at most two automatic reminders.
   There must be at least 2 days between sends. A later manual resend may
   therefore delay a scheduled reminder. Historical unsent invites are not
   automatically enrolled. Casey's explicitly requested invitation is included.
3. Reminders stop for accepted/completed, revoked or expired invitations.
   Failed/uncertain delivery is held for review, not repeatedly retried.
   First-guardian access and agency membership are checked again before sending.
   Queued approval mail starts its reminder clock only after transmission.
4. Each dependent's portal navigation includes Communication history, near
   Appointments. It lists recorded emails, texts, invitations, reminders and
   in-app notifications with recipient, channel, time and delivery status.
   Invitation history also shows individual attempts and the next reminder.
5. Both authorized guardians can see shared delivery progress. They cannot
   use this history to open the other's private message body, enrollment
   answers, signed private documents, account setup links or access tokens.
   "Sent" does not mean the recipient read the message.
