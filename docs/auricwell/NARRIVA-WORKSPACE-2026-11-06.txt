Auricwell Note Workspace and Narriva release — November 6, 2026

What is connected

Desktop Note Workspace and the primary mobile account open the existing session
and saved draft from the schedule or My Work. Manual Writer, Narriva and Smart
Notes share the editor and a per-user, per-workspace default. Existing account
restrictions on Manual Writer still apply. Older guided-builder drafts remain
readable. Smart Notes sends its answers through the authored Narriva generation
path rather than inventing normal findings, consent or an assessment.

Mobile receives masked client identities and age from the server. The condensed
profile provides authorized guardians and available Conversa contact actions.
Legacy Quick View PIN access does not receive primary-mobile clinical access.

Narriva Compliance runs five server-owned checks in this order: Risk & Safety;
Service Code & Documentation; Encounter Details & Consent; Clinical Necessity &
Interventions; Participation, Progress & Plan. Participant and crisis-service
reconciliation belongs to the service check. The UI displays actual persisted
progress, not a simulated approval animation. Unsupported facts remain findings.
Required risk assessment cannot be removed with a toggle or metadata edit.

Assessment evidence can be selected from authorized clean documents, uploaded
as protected session material, scanned, and previewed. Compliance receives only
an allowlisted evidence manifest; it does not read PDF contents. An unscanned or
quarantined upload cannot count as evidence or be opened as a clean attachment.

Verification binds the exact saved content, encounter facts, evidence versions,
provider qualifications, rules and authored guidance. Relevant edits invalidate
the result. A permitted bypass retains its finding and reason and cannot receive
Quick Sign. Existing author signatures, supervisor assignments, content hashes,
review tokens and immutable revisions remain authoritative. Amendments use their
existing version-specific signature actions, rather than Quick Sign.

Service corrections require the clinician's explicit confirmation and reason.
They synchronize the actual unsigned encounter and its source appointment,
drafts and queue items. A durable pending journal blocks generation/signing
between data-plane commits. A retry with the same confirmation can finish an
interrupted correction. This does not move a booking, send a notice, set a rate,
or automatically choose a crisis code.

Recording and privacy

Record Session lives on the actual session; Speak in the writer opens that
session. Required signers can receive an existing portal waiver or finger-sign
in the clinician-presented flow. The clinician cannot substitute their identity
for the client or guardian. The signed PDF and evidence must be stored before
recording is authorized, followed by a session-specific clinician confirmation.
Consent and authority are checked at start, resume and upload. Interruption,
locking, backgrounding and consent withdrawal pause or stop capture.

The existing approved private Cloud Speech/DLP/Vertex path processes encrypted
segments into a detailed editable Session Narrative. Identifying chart linkage
stays inside authorized application services. Narrative entry warns against
identifiers, blocks detected identifiers before model dispatch, and fails closed
when required privacy inspection or guidance is unavailable. Raw speech can
contain identifiers before transcription/redaction and is not anonymous.

Raw audio is temporary. A durable cleanup entry is created before upload; normal
processing deletes the object, with server retries for failed deletion. Retained
transcripts and narratives are encrypted. There is no retained-audio playback.
The storage operator must also enforce the temporary-audio lifecycle policy.

A completed narrative is offered separately. Insert/replace requires review and
does not silently overwrite clinician edits. Mobile lock recovery uses bounded
in-memory buffers scoped to the current account/workspace/source; it requires
fresh authorization and explicit restore/save. It is not durable offline storage
and does not survive process termination. Sign out/account change clears it.

Base H2011 and handbook information

Only base H2011 is added. H2011 ET is excluded. The October 2026 supplied manual
is the versioned reference, with source hash/pages and explicit unmapped states.
The service panel separates reference information from rates and agency/payer
restrictions. QBHA, bachelor-level and higher eligible qualifications follow the
base entry; a Peer Specialist-only or unknown classification does not acquire
base-code access. Existing disabled entries and explicit restrictions remain.

The Medicaid ladder starts at 8 minutes, reaches 16 units/247 minutes, and flags
248+ for manual service review with the manual's S9485 guidance. It does not
enable S9485, substitute codes or invent a rate. Scheduling, medical, credential,
payroll and future defaults are updated together.

An idempotent internal rollout adds the H-code handbook entry and an explicitly
targeted November 2026 addendum linked through existing update mechanisms.
Published history and unrelated unfinished handbook drafts remain preserved.
A conflicting draft is reported, while the independent November addendum can
publish. No staff messages or automatic distribution are sent.

Operations and rollout

Apply the new numbered migrations 1772, 1773 and 1774 through the existing
migration runner. Backend startup checks their successful log entries/storage
before exposing the release and runs the retry-safe internal handbook rollout.
Do not edit previously applied migrations or manually create partial tables.

Workspace feature_flags.narriva has independent writing, compliance, quickSign
and recording controls. Recording defaults off and requires an explicit true
for the authorized pilot; keep it disabled for ordinary usage until physical
device and live private-processing/deletion checks pass. An explicit false
disables the other features; do not disable note storage to stop capture.
Existing clinical permissions still apply.

Private processing requires the approved clinical privacy configuration,
project/service identity, encryption keys, DLP access, the private model path,
and the configured temporary audio bucket. CLINICAL_AI_PRIVACY_APPROVED is an
operator attestation and does not establish a contract or cloud approval.
CLINICAL_AUDIO_BUCKET (or the existing PTONBOARDFILES bucket fallback) must have
restricted service access and a lifecycle rule for temporary raw audio. Never
copy secrets into this document or logs. CLAMAV_SOCKET_PATH must point to the
approved scanner for newly uploaded assessments to become clean evidence.

Start testing at https://auricwell.com/mobile with an existing primary account.
Choose the correct workspace and open Today or My Work, then an actual session.
Use synthetic records first. Test all writer modes, save/reopen, backward
navigation, masking/contact permissions, compliance corrections and bypasses,
and an assigned supervisor's explicit review/sign action.

For iPhone Home Screen testing, open /mobile in Safari and use Share > Add to
Home Screen. This is browser installation; it does not update the native app.
For the native shell, use the existing com.auricwell.mobile Xcode project after
building the bundled mobile interface and running the Auricwell Capacitor sync.
Archive/sign with the authorized Apple developer team, upload to App Store
Connect and distribute through the existing TestFlight groups. Preserve the
existing push and associated-domain capabilities. A web deployment cannot
update an already installed app's bundled interface; install a fresh build.

Release validation must distinguish synthetic automated/browser checks from
physical-device and live-cloud checks. A signed iPhone build must exercise
microphone permission, pause/resume, phone interruptions, screen locking,
backgrounding, withdrawn consent, failed/retried chunks, multiple signers,
durable waiver PDFs, complete long-session narrative and raw-audio deletion.
Also test actual account lock/unlock and push deep links in that signed build.
Do not declare native capture or TestFlight distribution validated by a Vite
build or a desktop browser preview.

Rollback preserves drafts, historical notes, executed documents and review
history. Disable capture independently when necessary; never delete clinical
evidence to roll back the interface.

Completed release checks

The final focused Narriva matrix passed 294 tests, including real MySQL service
catalog/publication/correction cases, twice-applied review/preferences migration,
recording opt-in and consent, privacy canaries, attachment races, and model
executor compatibility. The established signature/claim suite passed 504 tests
with three pre-existing environment-dependent skips. Desktop Note Workspace
passed 238 tests; mobile/Quick View passed 76 tests. These suite counts overlap
in coverage and are not a count of unique application behaviors.

Production web and bundled mobile builds passed, and the Auricwell Capacitor
iOS sync passed. The first full web build exceeded Node's default heap; the
completed build used the existing 8 GiB build heap setting. Synthetic browser
previews exercised 320/390/430 px layouts without overflow. The native SDK check
still requests acceptance of the Xcode license, and DEVELOPMENT_TEAM is blank.
No signed archive, physical iPhone capture test or TestFlight upload is claimed.

Local gcloud configuration inspection requires account reauthentication. The
approved private-cloud/scanner configuration and actual processing/deletion
must therefore be checked during the explicit recording pilot. Deployment
workflow/readiness and public route checks are recorded separately in the
release deliverables, without accessing authenticated patient records.
