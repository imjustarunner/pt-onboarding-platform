# AuricWell implementation and release status

**Architecture correction in progress:** One shared backend serves the full-suite and AuricWell frontends. New practices and patient demographics now use the existing Agency/Client models; reviewed prototype conversion preserves IDs and references. New staff identities and appointments now use canonical shared records; existing staff support reviewed connections. Provider clinical/billing profiles, prototype appointment conversion, clinical notes, Note Aid and financial workflows still require consolidation. See [the authoritative architecture status](ARCHITECTURE.md).

This change adds an independently built Vue application in `auricwell/` and `/api/auricwell` routes mounted in the existing backend server. It is a development implementation, **not an approved TherapyNotes replacement launch**. No production migration, DNS change, deployment, claim transmission, customer invitation, or payment was performed during development.

## Product boundary

The `auricwell_ehr` designation and product configuration live in `auricwell_practices`, linked to the canonical `agencies` organization. New canonical organizations are clinical and inactive until remaining product/capability acceptance. Patient demographics live in `clients`; AuricWell retains access/provenance metadata and stable chart links. Separate product credentials/sessions remain. Staff identities and new appointment core fields use shared users/appointments. Provider clinical/billing profiles, older prototype appointments, clinical notes and financial records remain pending their shared-domain conversion. Existing organizations and clients are never matched automatically by name or slug.

The new backend reuses encryption, account-email delivery, Claim.MD payload/transport, Stripe, Gemini/Vertex, Vonage and PDF parsing libraries. It does not call the legacy school/payroll/lifecycle import orchestrator. Existing importer side effects and credentials remain separate. Signing is a new exact-version agreement workflow, not a hiring packet.

AuricWell product records use encryption with practice/kind/record-ID authenticated context. Canonical patient demographics now follow the existing `clients` schema, including its operational plaintext demographic columns; they are not represented as a separately encrypted AuricWell chart. Product SQL reads include practice IDs, and shared-client reads independently enforce the owning agency. HTTP sessions use a distinct opaque cookie and username credential store; no JWT/SSO fallback or shared login is provided. Staff require authenticator verification; patients can opt in. Invitations/recovery are the only outbound emails from this product. Notification items are generated inside the app.

## Implemented workflows

- Independent marketing, username login, MFA/recovery, practice chooser, white/blue/gold shell using the supplied AuricWell logo, responsive layouts, no practice skinning.
- Operator provisioning; saved new/transition setup; locations, timezone, therapy service fees; explicit permission and guardian financial-responsibility management. New practices never require migration files.
- Legal identity and service/order/BAA drafts; patient agreement templates; exact-version approval/issuance/signing, patient/guardian and optional practice countersignatures, revised versions, immutable retained executed PDF and UTF-8 source. PHI activation requires executed customer agreements and separate operator vendor/infrastructure evidence.
- Reviewed therapy provider profiles, supervision, locations, availability, inactive historical attribution, NPI/taxonomy and separate reviewed payer enrollment.
- Client charts, scheduled appointments/conflict detection, patient appointment requests, clinical drafts, linked billable notes, signatures/cosignatures, explicit patient sharing of historical pages.
- Day/week/month practice-timezone calendars and reviewed weekly, two-week or four-week recurring series (2–52 visits). All visits are checked for provider credentials, active clinical membership, supervision, availability and conflicts before atomic creation. Preview hashes reject changed schedules/configuration; command keys prevent duplicate commits. Individual edits and future-series rescheduling/cancellation preserve completed/past visits, linked notes/claims and individual exceptions, with retained change history. Missing/repeated daylight-saving wall times require correction. Existing bookings with inactive providers remain cancellable; clinical linkage cannot be rewritten.
- Practice-scoped Note Aid catalog and optional Vertex generation from clinician-supplied facts; drafts require clinician review and AI cannot sign or approve billing. Manual documentation works without AI.
- Practice-configurable clinical templates with text, choice and yes/no sections, service/type constraints, version snapshots in notes and required-field checks at signing. Administrators configure templates during non-PHI setup; clinicians use them independently of AI. Template revision/retirement preserves existing drafts and signed notes. Patient changes clear structured answers and narrative/title fields.
- Patient intake questionnaires with independently controlled capability, versioned assignments, explicit patient/guardian authority review, saved drafts, immutable submitted versions, correction requests and in-app tasks. Intake-only accounts can complete assigned forms before a chart exists. Assigned clinicians review the exact submitted version and explicitly create or link a chart; retries cannot create duplicate charts. Existing demographics, chart access and financial responsibility are preserved. Accepted intake responses follow clinical chart access; administrative queue access does not expose responses. This workflow uses authenticated, staff-assigned forms rather than public anonymous signup.
- Payers and electronic identity review, insurance/subscriber review, eligibility adapter, billing identity, draft claims, exact payload approval, reserved submission, uncertainty hold, clearinghouse response matching. Supervised rendering requires an explicitly reviewed payer/plan/date rule and exact supervisor cosign; no universal Colorado payer rule is inferred.
- Integer-cent manual ledger, opening balance separated from historical reports, Stripe connected-account payment reservation, reconciliation, refund reservation, manually reviewed remittance posting, patient responsibility, PDF statements/receipts. Financial external writes require an additional runtime practice allowlist.
- In-app secure messages, authorized recipients, read markers, reminders/notification list; video adapter checks patient/provider, visit time, vendor readiness and executed telehealth consent.
- CSV/XLSX reviewed imports for staff, payers, patients, billing history and future appointments. Private 1 MB resumable chunks up to 100 MB, SHA-256 originals, background text extraction and configured Vision OCR, patient/page review, unresolved historical attachments, row corrections/matching, reconciliation and conservative rollback that preserves edits/dependencies. Patient downloads contain reviewed page subsets, never the multi-patient original.

## Operational constraints

`AURICWELL_ENABLED` defaults off. Customer agreement renewal does not revoke already activated care-record access. Vendor failures hold new work; they do not rewrite signed documentation. Raw originals remain migration-only. Rollback is a reviewed withdrawal with audit provenance, not destructive deletion of edited clinical history.

Scheduling conflict and linked-record checks use keyset pagination across the practice’s records; production throughput remains unvalidated. Calendar display still uses the bounded workspace result. Record listing currently has bounded limits (500 by default; important chart/ledger lists use 10,000). Do not onboard a practice exceeding the validated limits until pagination and archive behavior are implemented and load-tested. Do not enable a production replacement practice solely because the UI, unit tests, or operator setup checks pass.

## Development without Docker

Docker was removed to conserve disk space and is not a prerequisite. `npm run test:auricwell` in `backend/` provisions a native temporary MySQL server, runs the isolated synthetic suite and removes its data afterward. All 60 backend tests, including PDF parsing/page isolation, template version/signing, patient intake and recurring scheduling checks, passed under Node 22 with native MySQL 9.5. The worker entry point also passes an empty-queue smoke check and exits cleanly after closing both database pools. Frontend dependencies have been restored and its build and four API/calendar checks pass.

Run the PDF worker directly with Node 22 and `npm run auricwell:imports`; run the independent built frontend with `npm start`. Hosting-specific artifacts and runtime acceptance still need validation on the selected deployment platform. The earlier Linux container image build failed and remains unverified; containers can be built remotely if the eventual host requires them. Neither Docker restoration nor container verification is a condition for continued local work.

## Note Aid interface and writer preservation

The existing full-suite Note Aid opens both sidebars on entry, including small screens, and collapses them when opening a draft, queue item or signed note. Each draft switch collapses them again without preventing manual expansion. Its writer prompts, clinical context assembly, post-processing and signing remain unchanged.

AuricWell now has a white three-column documentation workspace: drafts/records on the left, writing/review in the center and to-do/supervision on the right. Assigned-supervisor tasks open the complete signed content before exact-version cosigning. Past own-provider appointments without linked notes open an unsigned editor with reviewed patient/date/service defaults; they do not select billable status or create charges. New entry restores panels, editing collapses them, and practice changes remount the workspace. Intake, plan and discharge AI drafts retain the appropriate document type.

`clinicalNoteWriter.service.js` extracts the existing prompt builder and tool-specific generation settings without changing the full-suite writer contract. AuricWell reuses it with private transport, the existing section parser and incomplete-intake validation. Interrupted generation is not saved; patient/provider access is checked again before draft persistence. Shared prompt generation does not finish the canonical note/context/editor migration. See the explicit remaining scope in ARCHITECTURE.md.

Validation for this increment: 21 full-suite Note Aid view tests, 42 existing Note Aid backend regression checks, five shared-writer/privacy checks, 60 native AuricWell backend checks, four frontend tests and the AuricWell production build passed. Synthetic browser checks cover panel entry/edit/reopen behavior, switching drafts, assigned-supervisor cosigning, appointment prefilling, practice isolation and desktop/mobile layouts. Screenshots are `note-workspace-desktop.png`, `note-editor-desktop.png` and `note-workspace-mobile.png` in the verification directory. No live AI calls, patient data, financial transmission or deployment were used. Full financial-record consolidation remains outstanding.

## Shared insurance increment

AuricWell insurance review reads/writes the existing encrypted client insurance service with practice and canonical-client scope. The form loads current primary/subscriber coverage and patient billing identity, requires explicit review, submits the shared fingerprint and offers a reload after conflicts. Patient changes clear prior coverage immediately and ignore late responses. Secondary coverage and intake-profile links are retained when omitted from primary edits. These fields are billing identity; they do not silently replace clinical demographics.

Older AuricWell-only coverage is shown for comparison and cannot enter claim/eligibility processing until reviewed conversion. Conversion archives the original and replaces the active product payload with provenance metadata inside the same database transaction. There is no automatic policy merge. All claim review hashes now include shared coverage version, including changes to fields not displayed on the transmitted claim. Changes after approval block transmission before a clearinghouse request.

This increment reuses existing production insurance columns from migration 1413 and existing normalization/encryption. No new production migration is required; the native test schema projects the relevant existing columns. Canonical clinical notes/contexts, provider billing profiles, payer enrollment/authorizations, claims and ledger persistence still require further consolidation. Secondary insurance editing and the remaining revenue-cycle breadth are not completed by this increment.
