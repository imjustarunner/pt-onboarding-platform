APPLICATION CHANGE
Client traffic must select Notifyre, with the actual brand/campaign bound to
the sending agency. Missing configuration, disallowed purpose, wrong agency,
API failure or uncertain acceptance must never cause a Vonage fallback.
Staff notifications still select the approved Vonage staff sender.

Client replies received at the old Vonage number are retained in support's
communication review queue; they must not automatically confirm/cancel a
patient appointment or forward client text. Existing staff assistant and poll
replies remain available. STOP/HELP controls remain on the applicable carrier.

AuricWell settings now offer Notifyre or Off for client texting, and identify
account verification separately. A legacy Vonage clinical-enable flag cannot
reactivate patient texting. This addendum does not claim that the separate
inbox ownership/presence/topic work has been released.
