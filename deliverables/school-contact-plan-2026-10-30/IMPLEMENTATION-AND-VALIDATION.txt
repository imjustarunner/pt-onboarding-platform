BEHAVIOR

School-assigned providers see an optional welcome, choose Yes or No, and can
reopen a minimized card. The next screen previews their introduction, allows
plain-text editing with four required placeholders, sends examples, and saves
follow-up timing. Sending an example minimizes the plan and preserves the draft;
non-SSO providers have a Check Messages link. Enabled plans can be turned off.
Newly mandated providers are prompted again even if previously declined.

Defaults are SMS3 days/email7 days from the initial email, and stale parent
response72 hours. SMS ranges1–30 days, later email2–60 days, stale1–336 hours.
Only school assignments created after opt-in enroll. Existing assignments and
no-op assignment edits do not send to the old caseload. New work is queued in
the assignment transaction and delivered after commit by the minute worker.
Repeated updates/retries do not duplicate accepted messages.

Every successful automatic client contact uses the normal communication record
and encrypted contact note, records an automation label, and updates the existing
SchoolCareBridge checklist/count. The later email adds a distinct attempt even
in the initial email conversation. Failed/queued sends and examples do not count.
The client area includes workflow status and an outside-reply stop action.

From uses provider display name with messages@. SSO uses a messages+token Reply-To
for routing into the same app thread; non-SSO uses the configured personal email.
Intended provider copies are recorded; physical pilot To/Cc/Bcc are restricted
to testing@. A demo guardian explicitly linked with testing@ is required to test
human replies; there is no general remapping of arbitrary testing-inbox replies.

Human parent email/text replies stop pending family follow-ups. External non-SSO
replies need the provider's Record parent reply action if the app did not receive
them. A waiting parent response triggers one support in-app alert and provider
email, plus a provider text only when its fixture and staff consent permit it.
The first unanswered parent response starts the stale timer; repeat messages do
not postpone it. Provider responses or a recorded first service stop that workflow. Plan/assignment/access
and enrollment-window checks are repeated before delivery.

Ambiguous transport failures enter needs_review and are not blindly resent.
Reconcile the original sent receipt, RFC Message-ID, and stored
from_json.schoolContactAutomation before any operator reset. Stable prepared
IDs use <school-contact-{workflowId}-{stepKey}@itsco.health>. A disabled/re-enabled
plan does not resurrect work from a prior enrollment window.
