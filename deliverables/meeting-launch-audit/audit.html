Compatibility retained intentionally

  • Existing room-token columns and old calendar/bookmark routes remain for compatibility. They are not invitations to share and do not independently grant ordinary meeting access.
  • Personal invitation tokens still validate the recipient account and current meeting roster on every resolution. Forwarding a personal invitation does not impersonate its recipient.
  • Interview candidate guest links and clinical client/guardian invitations serve external participants and are distinct from obsolete anonymous staff-supervision access.
  • Vonage project-key compatibility and historically named roomSid/twilio_room_sid storage remain: the active video adapter uses Vonage. Removing these names without migrating stored sessions could break existing meetings.
  • Legacy stored reminder settings remain supported without bypassing notification opt-outs. Existing records are not bulk-emailed by this change.
  • Recording-rule provider functions remain unimplemented and the recording-rules endpoint fails rather than reporting success. No caller was found in the current meeting UI; recording is not certified by this audit.
