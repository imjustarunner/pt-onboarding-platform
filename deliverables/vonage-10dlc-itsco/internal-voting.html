Campaign name

ITSCO Staff Notifications and Voting

Description

ITSCO, LLC, known as ITSCO, sends staff announcements, supervisor-to-team administrative messages, schedule and meeting notifications, portal and session-access links, and optional nonpolitical internal polls, event attendance requests and date or activity selections. Authorized organizers choose the question and answer options and send to selected staff groups. Staff reply with Y/N or numbered choices; an event code identifies the poll when multiple polls are open. Responses are recorded for the organizer. Staff can view final aggregate results in the app after voting closes and separately request a results text for each poll. Recipients are opted-in employees and contractors participating in this organization’s internal events. Messages are limited to this organization’s program and each recipient’s consent. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. No promotional marketing. No political elections, fundraising, patient information or messaging on behalf of independent customer businesses.

Public-review and activation checks

  • Open the consent, terms and privacy URLs in a signed-out browser after publishing. The example must identify ITSCO and offer separate unselected Yes/No polling and workforce choices; it cannot enroll visitors.
  • Use a dedicated staff notifications and polling number. Do not move an existing service number to this campaign.
  • Existing appointment/workforce consent is not automatically polling consent. Collect each staff member’s signed polling and workforce choices independently.
  • Company Events → RSVP / voting → Custom vote supports question and choice keys. Use Y=Yes and N=No, or 1/2/3 with your own labels. Select the intended staff groups or individuals.
  • Set a unique event code such as WORKSHOP. When two polls are open, use WORKSHOP Y or DATES 2.
  • In Dashboard → Today’s Events → Staff polls and results, staff can vote and optionally select “Text me the final results.” Closing voting reveals aggregate totals and sends results only to staff who requested them and still have valid polling SMS consent. No automatic resend is attempted after an ambiguous or failed delivery; check Company Events → Delivery.
  • Company Events → Send message supports staff individuals, groups and role audiences. General announcements use workforce consent. Supervisor status alone does not grant agency-wide event-management permission; the sender must have the appropriate existing staff/admin permission.
  • Test receipt, eligible-group access, saved responses, confirmation SMS, HELP and STOP before rollout.
