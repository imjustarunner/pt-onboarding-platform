APPOINTMENT AND SIGN-UP MESSAGE WORDING — NEW ADDENDUM
October 27, 2026

These are shared application defaults. Agencies will use them when messaging is enabled; this work does not turn on automatic sending for additional agencies. The reminder worker remains restricted to demo agency 450.

1. Use local appointment times
Standard appointment messages show the appointment's local date, time and zone, for example Thursday, October 29 at 1:00 PM MDT. They must not display raw Date.toString() output such as 19:50:00 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time).
The shared send path also recognizes old queued standard messages and formats their dates at delivery. This avoids requiring a separate queue cleanup when another agency is enabled. Agency-authored custom messages retain their wording.

2. Confirmation after a Y reply
Thanks! Your appointment is confirmed. If you need to make any changes, please send us a message. A team member will get back to you during our regular business hours.
Use plain punctuation so the previous em dash does not appear as a question mark on the phone.

3. One sign-up confirmation naming the enabled message types
A family selecting care messages and reminders receives:
ITSCO: You'll receive appointment reminders and messages from your care team. Frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. STOP to stop, HELP for help.

Include START to restart only when the registered program supports it.
Use the registered agency brand rather than hardcoding ITSCO for other agencies.
If billing is selected, include billing updates. Staff choices identify staff updates or staff message alerts and surveys as applicable. Practice announcements and account security messages are named only if selected.

Family sign-up previously confirmed each purpose separately. It now shares the existing batch enrollment behavior: one confirmation per completed signed request, while each Yes/No choice remains recorded separately. Reviewing the same activated request again sends no new confirmation. All-No sends no acceptance text. Separate sign-ups or recipient phones remain separate enrollments; this does not merge identities or signed consent records.
No new sign-up texts were sent merely to demonstrate this wording to the owner, and no existing signed consents were changed.

Required bot checks on the next fresh enrollment
- Choose two or three message types; verify one combined confirmation naming exactly those types.
- Decline a type and verify it is absent from the confirmation and remains disabled.
- Review the same completed request again; verify no duplicate text.
- Choose No for all types; verify no acceptance message.
- Verify STOP still prevents sending; the new batch must never clear a prior STOP.
- Verify a failed confirmation leaves the newly accepted types inactive and records the failure for review.
- Capture the actual phone message when possible. Automated tests verify the logic; they do not establish carrier or handset delivery for this new wording.
