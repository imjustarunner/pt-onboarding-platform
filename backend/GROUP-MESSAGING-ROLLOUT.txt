GROUP MESSAGING AND TICKETS — 2026-11-05

Groups sits beside Email; the former Internal channel is labeled Direct message.
Groups includes current departmental queues and member-created internal groups.
Personal non-SSO login Groups remain personal inboxes.

Ticketed departments: Payroll, Billing, Credentialing, Support, Administration,
Technology, Compliance, and Medical Records where agency senders are configured.
People Operations is explicitly excluded from this rollout. Its existing group,
subscriptions, hiring permissions, and existing ticket workflows are not enrolled
in the new departmental synchronization or sender provisioning.

Agency payroll/billing/credentialing permissions grant the matching department;
Support grants Support and Technology. Agency admins receive the departmental
administrative queues, including Compliance. No rights come from a matching job
title or personal email address. The API checks active agency grants on reads,
claims, assignment, and sends; nested non-SSO Group accounts use the same grants.

Departmental intake creates/attaches a ticket. The email reader stays read only
and opens the existing ticket interface in a modal. Claiming removes shared
unread attention and keeps the item in the claimant's Assigned to me view. The
existing dedicated Technology intake/assignment rules remain in place.

SENDING AS THE GROUP
Membership sync uses NONE delivery for people and nested staff Groups; only the
app transport continues ingesting department mail. It also provisions/verifies
the exact department From address on the app's configured Gmail send-as or SMTP
relay transport. This grants sending inside the app under current permissions,
without adding aliases to individual members' personal Gmail accounts.
Unaccepted/mismatched senders are reported as errors and retried by recurring
reconciliation. The outbound verifier refuses unverified From addresses.

ROLLOUT
The existing membership worker reconciles configured departmental identities;
role and agency changes queue prompt reconciliation. To review existing state,
run from backend using the deployment's normal database/Workspace environment:
  node src/scripts/reconcileDepartmentalGroups.js --agency=<agency-id>
To apply membership plus transport authorization:
  node src/scripts/reconcileDepartmentalGroups.js --agency=<agency-id> --apply
The report includes sender.ready and sender.transport. Preview mode does not
provision senders or change Google membership.

Existing unticketed plaintext histories have a separate bounded preview/apply:
  node src/scripts/backfillDepartmentalEmailTickets.js --agency=<agency-id>
  node src/scripts/backfillDepartmentalEmailTickets.js --agency=<agency-id> --apply
Use nextAfterId with --after=<id> for subsequent batches. Rich HTML, unfinished
drafts, unsent mail, manually attached tickets, existing receipt/thread conflicts,
and missing attachment storage are left untouched for review. Eligible migration
verifies encrypted history before replacing plaintext mirror bodies. It retains
claimants, sent history, internal notes, and attachment storage references.
