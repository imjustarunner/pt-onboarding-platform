Stedi coordination of benefits (COB)
===================================

Purpose and scope
Billing staff at ITSCO (2), Next Level Up (6), and Inner Strength (377) can open
Check overlapping coverage from the private insurance editor. COB investigates
overlapping coverage using a saved, verified, participating policy. Find insurance
remains the separate discovery action for unknown coverage. Claim.MD continues
to handle ordinary eligibility, claims, and ERAs.

Connection
- Use the production STEDI_API_KEY server secret, shared with discovery.
- STEDI_COB_ENABLED=true enables this separate feature. It defaults off.
- STEDI_DISCOVERY_ENABLED is independent. Keys are never sent to the browser.
- The production key is managed in Google Secret Manager, project ptonboard-dev,
  secret STEDI_API_KEY. The Cloud Run runtime identity needs access to that secret.
- A read-only Stedi payer-directory request can validate authentication without
  sending patient data or creating a paid eligibility/COB request.
- No enrollment, live patient checks, batch checks, or claims are triggered by
  deploying this feature, viewing history, or saving the key alone.

Staff flow
1. Save and verify the existing policy, client and subscriber identities.
2. Open the COB panel and choose primary or secondary saved coverage.
3. Check participation. Only the saved insurer name is sent to the Stedi payer
   directory. Select the exact participating insurer/plan entity; the server
   independently verifies current COB support before reserving the paid check.
4. Choose an agency-owned billing office with a practice name and valid group
   NPI, a service date within the past two years through today, and a reason.
5. Confirm legal name spelling, birthdates, address, active policy/member ID,
   selected payer, and charge. The COB API receives provider, subscriber,
   dependent when needed, service date, and service type 30. Address confirmation
   is a staff accuracy gate; address and SSN are not sent in this COB request.
6. Submit once. Stedi's listed starting price checked November 10, 2026 is $1
   per COB check. Account pricing applies. There is no daily or monthly hard cap.

Duplicate and transport safeguards
Migration 1791 adds a request_kind to the existing encrypted Stedi ledger. Older
rows default to discovery. Both products use the same monthly usage lock and
client lock; each new attempt is committed before its network request. Matching
request keys always reuse the existing row. Same policy, selected payer, identity,
and service date within 30 days reuse the saved result unless staff explicitly
request a fresh paid check and explain why. There is no automatic POST retry.
An unresolved COB attempt prevents another new paid COB attempt until reconciled.
Unresolved discovery matches can still be investigated with COB. Read-only
directory requests do not increment the usage counter.

Results and billing safety
The full response, including unfamiliar fields, is encrypted and retained with
the billing profile. Structured findings show payer names, reported member IDs,
coverage dates, service types, reported order, and identity consistency.
COB entity payer IDs are proprietary, not Claim.MD IDs or Stedi routing IDs.
Copying a policy to an empty form leaves both routing IDs blank and coverage
unverified. Existing policies are never overwritten and billing order is never
silently changed.

A consistent MemberFoundNoCob response with matching subscriber/client identity,
matching member ID, active coverage, no errors, and no other saved policy needs
no extra COB resolution. It does not prove that other coverage does not exist.
It is stored with status clear and preserves an otherwise valid coverage review.
Overlap, unknown order, errors, missing fields, or identity discrepancies create
an urgent billing follow-up and a durable hold shared by claim readiness and
collection checks. Coverage review cannot bypass that hold. New findings and
their resolution invalidate earlier coverage reviews. Medicaid-first guards remain.

Billing staff resolve each reported policy, save verified coverage and Claim.MD
routing, confirm billing order against payer evidence, and record the reference.
Recorded positions must match an order explicitly returned by Stedi. A payer
correction or inapplicable finding needs a documented explanation. Ambiguous
transport outcomes are reconciled using the Stedi portal, without another POST.
Third/additional policies remain in the full response and findings; this feature
does not expand the insurance editor beyond primary/secondary. A third policy
that applies requires billing follow-up rather than silently dropping coverage.

Limitations
Only participating plans can be queried. Original Medicare is not supported;
some Medicare Advantage plans are. COB data can be incomplete or negative even
when other coverage exists. Stedi may not determine primacy. Unknown order must
be confirmed with the payers. No-result responses never remove saved coverage.
